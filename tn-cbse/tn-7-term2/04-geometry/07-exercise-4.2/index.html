Note

We learnt the congruency criterions of triangles. The following ordered combinations of measurement of triangles will not be sufficient to prove congruency of triangles.

Angle - Angle - Angle (AAA) combination will not always prove triangles congruent. This combination will give triangles of same shape but not of same size.

Side - Side - Angle (or) Angle - Side - Side (SSA or ASS) combination also will not prove congruency of triangles. This combination deals with two sides and a non-included angle.

Try these

  1. If \(\triangle ABC \cong \triangle XYZ\) then list the corresponding sides and corresponding angles.
  2. Given triangles are congruent. Identify the corresponding parts and write the congruent statement.
  3. Mention the conditions needed to conclude the congruency of the triangles with reference to the above said criterions. Give reasons for your answer.
  1. Given that \(\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF\) (i) List all the corresponding congruent sides (ii) List all the corresponding congruent angles.
  2. If the given two triangles are congruent, then identify all the corresponding sides and also write the congruent angles.
  3. If the given triangles \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle EFG\) are congruent, determine whether the given pair of sides and angles are corresponding sides or corresponding angles or not.
    1. \(\angle A\) and \(\angle G\)
    2. \(\angle B\) and \(\angle E\)
    3. \(\angle B\) and \(\angle G\)
    4. \(\overline{AC}\) and \(\overline{GF}\)
    5. \(\overline{BA}\) and \(\overline{FG}\)
    6. \(\overline{EF}\) and \(\overline{BC}\)
  4. State whether the two triangles are congruent or not. Justify your answer.
  5. To conclude the congruency of triangles, mark the required information in the following figures with reference to the given congruency criterion.
  6. For each pair of triangles state the criterion that can be used to determine the congruency?
    1. Construct a triangle \(XYZ\) with the given conditions.
      1. \(XY = 6.4\text{ cm}\), \(ZY = 7.7\text{ cm}\) and \(XZ = 5\text{ cm}\)
      2. An equilateral triangle of side \(7.5\text{ cm}\)
      3. An isosceles triangle with equal sides \(4.6\text{ cm}\) and third side \(6.5\text{ cm}\)
    2. Construct a triangle \(ABC\) with given conditions.
      1. \(AB = 7\text{ cm}\), \(AC = 6.5\text{ cm}\) and \(\angle A = 120^{\circ}\).
      2. \(BC = 8\text{ cm}\), \(AC = 6\text{ cm}\) and \(\angle C = 40^{\circ}\).
      3. An isosceles obtuse triangle with equal sides \(5\text{ cm}\)
    3. Construct a triangle \(PQR\) with given conditions.
      1. \(\angle P = 60^{\circ}\), \(\angle R = 35^{\circ}\) and \(PR = 7.8\text{ cm}\)
      2. \(\angle P = 115^{\circ}\), \(\angle Q = 40^{\circ}\) and \(PQ = 6\text{ cm}\)
      3. \(\angle Q = 90^{\circ}\), \(\angle R = 42^{\circ}\) and \(QR = 5.5\text{ cm}\)

Objective type questions

  1. If two plane figures are congruent then they have
    1. same size
    2. same shape
    3. same angle
    4. same shape and same size
  2. Which of the following methods are used to check the congruence of plane figures?
    1. translation method
    2. superposition method
    3. substitution method
    4. transposition method
  3. Which of the following rule is not sufficient to verify the congruency of two triangles.
    1. SSS rule
    2. SAS rule
    3. SSA rule
    4. ASA rule
  4. Two students drew a line segment each. What is the condition for them to be congruent?
    1. They should be drawn with a scale.
    2. They should be drawn on the same sheet of paper.
    3. They should have different lengths.
    4. They should have the same length.
  5. In the given figure, \(AD = CD\) and \(AB = CB\). Identify the other three pairs that are equal.
    1. \(\angle ADB = \angle CDB\), \(\angle ABD = \angle CBD\), \(BD = BD\)
    2. \(AD = AB\), \(DC = CB\), \(BD = BD\)
    3. \(AB = CD\), \(AD = BC\), \(BD = BD\)
    4. \(\angle ADB = \angle CDB\), \(\angle ABD = \angle CBD\), \(\angle DAB = \angle DBC\)
  6. In \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle PQR\), \(\angle A = 50^{\circ} = \angle P\), \(PQ = AB\), and \(PR = AC\). By which property \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle PQR\) are congruent?
    1. SSS property
    2. SAS property
    3. ASA property
    4. RHS property
