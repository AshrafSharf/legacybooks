  1. Can \(30^{\circ}\), \(60^{\circ}\) and \(90^{\circ}\) be the angles of a triangle?
  2. Can you draw a triangle with \(25^{\circ}\), \(65^{\circ}\) and \(80^{\circ}\) as angles?
  3. In each of the following triangles, find the value of \(x\).
  1. Two line segments \(\overline{AD}\) and \(\overline{BC}\) intersect at \(O\). Joining \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{DC}\) we get two triangles, \(\triangle AOB\) and \(\triangle DOC\) as shown in the figure. Find the \(\angle A\) and \(\angle B\).
  1. Observe the figure and find the value of \(\angle A + \angle N + \angle G + \angle L + \angle E + \angle S\).
  2. If the three angles of a triangle are in the ratio \(3:5:4\), then find them.
  3. In \(\triangle RST\), \(\angle S\) is \(10^{\circ}\) greater than \(\angle R\) and \(\angle T\) is \(5^{\circ}\) less than \(\angle S\), find the three angles of the triangle.
  4. In \(\triangle ABC\), if \(\angle B\) is 3 times \(\angle A\) and \(\angle C\) is 2 times \(\angle A\), then find the angles.
  5. In \(\triangle XYZ\), if \(\angle X : \angle Z\) is \(5 : 4\) and \(\angle Y = 72^{\circ}\). Find \(\angle X\) and \(\angle Z\)
  6. In a right angled triangle \(ABC\), \(\angle B\) is right angle, \(\angle A\) is \(x + 1\) and \(\angle C\) is \(2x + 5\). Find \(\angle A\) and \(\angle C\).
  7. In a right angled triangle \(MNO\), \(\angle N = 90^{\circ}\), \(MO\) is extended to \(P\). If \(\angle NOP = 128^{\circ}\), find the other two angles of \(\triangle MNO\).
  8. Find the value of \(x\) in each of the given triangles.
  1. In \(\triangle LMN\), \(MN\) is extended to \(O\). If \(\angle MLN = 100 - x\), \(\angle LMN = 2x\) and \(\angle LNO = 6x - 5\), find the value of \(x\).

Objective type questions

  1. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio \(2:3:4\). Then the angles are

    (i) 20, 30, 40    (ii) 40, 60, 80    (iii) 80, 20, 80    (iv) 10, 15, 20

  2. One of the angles of a triangle is \(65^{\circ}\). If the difference of the other two angles is \(45^{\circ}\), then the two angles are

    (i) \(85^{\circ}, 40^{\circ}\)    (ii) \(70^{\circ}, 25^{\circ}\)    (iii) \(80^{\circ}, 35^{\circ}\)    (iv) \(80^{\circ}, 135^{\circ}\)

  1. In the given figure, \(AB\) is parallel to \(CD\). Then the value of \(b\) is

    (i) \(112^{\circ}\)    (ii) \(68^{\circ}\)    (iii) \(102^{\circ}\)    (iv) \(62^{\circ}\)

  1. In the given figure, which of the following statement is true?

    (i) \(x + y + z = 180^{\circ}\)    (ii) \(x + y + z = a + b + c\)

    (iii) \(x + y + z = 2(a + b + c)\)    (iv) \(x + y + z = 3(a + b + c)\)

  2. An exterior angle of a triangle is \(70^{\circ}\) and two interior opposite angles are equal. Then measure of each of these angle will be

    (i) \(110^{\circ}\)    (ii) \(120^{\circ}\)    (iii) \(35^{\circ}\)    (iv) \(60^{\circ}\)

  1. In a \(\triangle ABC\), \(AB = AC\). The value of \(x\) is \(\underline{\qquad}\).

    (i) \(80^{\circ}\)    (ii) \(100^{\circ}\)

    (iii) \(130^{\circ}\)    (iv) \(120^{\circ}\)

  2. If an exterior angle of a triangle is \(115^{\circ}\) and one of the interior opposite angles is \(35^{\circ}\), then the other two angles of the triangle are

    (i) \(45^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}\)    (ii) \(65^{\circ}, 80^{\circ}\)    (iii) \(65^{\circ}, 70^{\circ}\)    (iv) \(115^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}\)
