Miscellaneous Practice problems

  1. In an isosceles triangle one angle is \(76^{\circ}\). If the other two angles are equal find them.
  2. If two angles of a triangle are \(46^{\circ}\) each, how can you classify the triangle?
  3. If one angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the other two angles, find the type of the triangle.
  4. If the exterior angle of a triangle is \(140^{\circ}\) and its interior opposite angles are equal, find all the interior angles of the triangle.
  5. In \(\triangle JKL\), if \(\angle J = 60^{\circ}\) and \(\angle K = 40^{\circ}\), then find the value of exterior angle formed by extending the side \(KL\).
  6. Find the value of ‘\(x\)’ in the given figure.
  7. If \(\triangle MNO \cong \triangle DEF\), \(\angle M = 60^{\circ}\) and \(\angle E = 45^{\circ}\) then find the value of \(\angle O\).
  8. In the given figure ray \(AZ\) bisects \(\angle BAD\) and \(\angle DCB\), prove that (i) \(\triangle BAC \cong \triangle DAC\) (ii) \(AB = AD\).
  9. In the given figure \(FG = FI\) and \(H\) is midpoint of \(GI\), prove that \(\triangle FGH \cong \triangle FHI\)
  10. Using the given figure, prove that the triangles are congruent. Can you conclude that \(AC\) is parallel to \(DE\).
