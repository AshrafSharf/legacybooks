Chapter 3 · Algebra

Exercise 3.3

Textbook pages 20–21

  1. Fill in the blanks.
    1. The degree of the term \(a^{3}b^{2}c^{4}d^{2}\) is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    2. Degree of the constant term is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    3. The coefficient of leading term of the expression \(3z^{2}y + 2x - 3\) is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
  2. Say True or False.
    1. The degree of \(m^{2}n\) and \(mn^{2}\) are equal.
    2. \(7a^{2}b\) and \(-7ab^{2}\) are like terms.
    3. The degree of the expression \(-4x^{2}yz\) is \(-4\).
    4. Any integer can be the degree of the expression.
  3. Find the degree of the following terms.
    1. \(5x^{2}\)
    2. \(-7ab\)
    3. \(12pq^{2}r^{2}\)
    4. \(-125\)
    5. \(3z\)
  4. Find the degree of the following expressions.
    1. \(x^{3} - 1\)
    2. \(3x^{2} + 2x + 1\)
    3. \(3t^{4} - 5st^{2} + 7s^{3}t^{2}\)
    4. \(5 - 9y + 15y^{2} - 6y^{3}\)
    5. \(u^{5} + u^{4}v + u^{3}v^{2} + u^{2}v^{3} + uv^{4}\)
  5. Identify the like terms : \(12x^{3}y^{2}z,\ -y^{3}x^{2}z,\ 4z^{3}y^{2}x,\ 6x^{3}z^{2}y,\ -5y^{3}x^{2}z\)
  6. Add and find the degree of the following expressions.
    1. \((9x + 3y)\) and \((10x - 9y)\)
    2. \((k^{2} - 25k + 46)\) and \((23 - 2k^{2} + 21k)\)
    3. \((3m^{2}n + 4pq^{2})\) and \((5nm^{2} - 2q^{2}p)\)
  7. Simplify and find the degree of the following expressions.
    1. \(10x^{2} - 3xy + 9y^{2} - (3x^{2} - 6xy - 3y^{2})\)
    2. \(9a^{4} - 6a^{3} - 6a^{4} - 3a^{2} + 7a^{3} + 5a^{2}\)
    3. \(4x^{2} - 3x - [8x - (5x^{2} - 8)]\)

Objective type questions

  1. \(3p^{2} - 5pq + 2q^{2} + 6pq - q^{2} + pq\) is a
    1. Monomial
    2. Binomial
    3. Trinomial
    4. Quadrinomial
  2. The degree of \(6x^{7} - 7x^{3} + 4\) is
    1. 7
    2. 3
    3. 6
    4. 4
  3. If \(p(x)\) and \(q(x)\) are two expressions of degree 3, then the degree of \(p(x) + q(x)\) is
    1. 6
    2. 0
    3. 3
    4. Undefined
