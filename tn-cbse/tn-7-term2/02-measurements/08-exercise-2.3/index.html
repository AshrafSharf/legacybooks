Example 2.21 A Kho-Kho ground has dimensions \(30 m \times 19 m\) which includes a lobby on all of its sides. The dimensions of the playing area is \(27 m \times 16 m\). Find the area of the lobby.

Solution

From the dimensions of the ground we have,

\(L = 30 m\); \(B =19 m\); \(l = 27 m\) ; \(b =16 m\)

Area of the kho kho ground \(= L \times B\)

\(= 30 \times 19\)

\(= 570 m\)

Area of the play field \(= l \times b\)

\(= 27 \times 16\)

\(= 432 m\) Fig. 2.25

Area of the lobby = Area of Kho-Kho ground - Area of the play field
