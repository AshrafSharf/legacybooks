Objective type questions

  1. ________ is a representative value of the entire data.
  2. Mean (ii) range (iii) minimum value (iv) maximum value
  3. The mean of first fifteen even numbers is _______
  4. 4 (ii) 16 (iii) 5 (iv) 10
  5. The average of two numbers are 20. One number is 24, another number is ________
  6. 16 (ii) 26 (iii) 20 (iv) 40
  7. The mean of the data 12, x, 28 is 18. Find the value of x.
  8. 18 (ii) 16 (iii) 14 (iv) 22
