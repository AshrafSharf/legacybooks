Exercise 5.3

Textbook pages 16 · 1 figure

  1. Fill in the blanks.
    1. The median of the data 12, 14, 23, 25, 34, 11, 42, 45, 32, 22, 44 is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    2. The median of first ten even natural numbers is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
  2. Find the median of the given data: 35, 25, 34, 36, 45, 18, 28.
  3. The weekly sale of motor bikes in a showroom for the past 14 weeks given below. 10, 6, 8, 3, 5, 6, 4, 7, 12, 13, 16, 10, 4, 7. Find the median of the data.
  4. Find the median of the 10 observations 36, 33, 45, 28, 39, 45, 54, 23, 56, 25. If another observation 35 is added to the above data, what would be the new median?

Objective type questions

  1. If the median of \(a,\ 2a,\ 4a,\ 6a,\ 9a\) is 8, then find the value of \(a\) is

    (i) 8    (ii) 6    (iii) 2    (iv) 10

  2. The median of the data 24, 29, 34, 38, 35 and 30, is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\)

    (i) 29    (ii) 30    (iii) 34    (iv) 32

  3. The median first 6 odd natural numbers is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\)

    (i) 6    (ii) 7    (iii) 8    (iv) 14
