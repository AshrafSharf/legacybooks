1.8 Relationship between the Numbers and their HCF and LCM

Textbook pages 19–20 · 1 figure

Let us find the HCF and the LCM of 36 and 48. First, find the factors of 36 and 48 using division method.

\(36 = 2 x 2 x 3 x 3\); \(48 = 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 x 3 2 36 2 48 HCF = 2 x 2 x 3 = 12 2 18 2 24 LCM = 2 x 2 x 3 x 2 x 2 x 3 =144 3 9 2 12\)

Observe that, \(36 x 48 = 144 x 12 = 1728 3 3 2 6\) We find that, 1 3 3 product of two given numbers = their HCF x LCM 1 In general, for any 2 numbers x and y,

\(x \times y = HCF\) (x, y) \(\times LCM\) (x, y)

Numbers

Example 12: The LCM of two numbers is 432 and their HCF is 36. If one of the numbers is 108, then find the other number. Solution: We know that, the product of the two numbers=LCM x HCF

108 x (the other number) \(= 432 x 36\)

The other number \(= (432 x 36) \div 108 = 144\)

Example 13: The LCM of two co-prime numbers is 5005. If one of the numbers is 65, then find the other number. Solution: We know that, the product of the two numbers \(= LCM x HCF\) As the HCF of co-primes is 1, 65 x (the other number) \(= 5005 x 1\) The other number \(= 5005 \div 65 = 77\)
