Exercise 2.1

Textbook pages 9–10 · 7 figures

  1. Fill in the blanks.
    1. The letters \(a, b, c, \ldots\, x, y, z\) are used to represent \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    2. The algebraic statement of '\(f\) decreased by 5' is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    3. The algebraic statement of '\(s\) divided by 5' is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    4. If A's age is '\(n\)' years now, 7 years ago A's age was \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    5. If '\(p - 5\)' gives 12 then '\(p\)' is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
  2. Say True or False.
    1. The length of part B in the pencil shown is '\(a - 6\)'.
    2. If the cost of an is '\(x\)' and the cost of a is ₹5, then the total cost of fruits is ₹ '\(x + 5\)'.
    3. 10 more to three times '\(c\)' is '\(10c + 3\)'
    4. If the cost of 10 rice bags is ₹ '\(t\)', then the cost of 1 rice bag is ₹\(\frac{t}{10}\).
    5. The product of '\(q\)' and 20 is '\(20q\)'.
  3. Draw the next two patterns and complete the table.
  4. Arivazhagan is 30 years younger to his father. Write Arivazhagan's age in terms of his father's age.
  5. If '\(u\)' is an even number, how would you represent
    1. the next even number?
    2. the previous even number?
  6. Express the following verbal statement to algebraic statement.
    1. '\(t\)' is added to 100.
    2. 4 times '\(q\)'.
    3. 4 less to 9 times of '\(y\)'.
  7. Express the following algebraic statement to verbal statement.
    1. \(x \div 3\)
    2. \(11 + 10x\)
    3. \(70s\)
  8. The teacher asked two students to write the algebraic statement for the verbal statement "8 more than a number". Vetri wrote '\(8 + x\)' but Maran wrote '\(8x\)'. Who gave the correct answer?
  9. Answer the following questions.
    1. If '\(g\)' is equal to 300 what is the value of '\(g - 1\)' and '\(g + 1\)'?
    2. What is the value of '\(s\)', if '\(2s - 6\)' gives 30?
  10. Complete the table and find the value of '\(k\)' for which \(\frac{k}{3}\) gives 5.

Objective Type Questions

  1. Variable means that it
    (a) can take only a few values    (b) has a fixed value
    (c) can take different values    (d) can take only 8 values
  2. The number of days in '\(w\)' weeks is
    (a) \(30 + w\)    (b) \(30w\)    (c) \(7 + w\)    (d) \(7w\)
  3. The value of '\(x\)' in the circle is (a) 6    (b) 8    (c) 21    (d) 22
  4. The value of '\(y\)' in \(y + 7 = 13\) is
    (a) \(y = 5\)    (b) \(y = 6\)    (c) \(y = 7\)    (d) \(y = 8\)
  5. 6 less to '\(n\)' gives 8 is represented as
    (a) \(n - 6 = 8\)    (b) \(6 - n = 8\)    (c) \(8 - n = 6\)    (d) \(n - 8 = 6\)
