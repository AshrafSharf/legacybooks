Chapter 3 · Ratio Proportion

Exercise 3.1

Textbook pages 6–7 · 3 figures

  1. Fill in the blanks.
    1. Ratio of 5 k.m 400 m to 6 k.m = \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    2. Ratio of 75 paise to ₹2 = \(\underline{\qquad}\).
  2. Say whether the following statements are True or False.
    1. The ratio of 130 c.m to 1 m is \(13 : 10\).
    2. One of the terms in a ratio cannot be 1.
  3. Find the simplified form of the following ratios.
    (i) \(15 : 20\) (ii) \(32 : 24\) (iii) \(7 : 15\) (iv) \(12 : 27\) (v) \(75 : 100\)
  4. Akilan walks 10 k.m in an hour while Selvi walks 6 km in an hour. Find the simplest ratio of the distance covered by Akilan to that of Selvi.
  5. The cost of parking a bicycle is ₹5 and the cost of parking a scooter is ₹15. Find the simplest ratio of the parking cost of a bicycle to that of a scooter.
  6. Out of 50 students in a class, 30 are boys. Find the ratio of
    1. number of boys to the number of girls.
    2. number of girls to the total number of students.
    3. number of boys to the total number of students.

Objective Type Questions

  1. The ratio of ₹1 to 20 paise is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    (a) \(1 : 5\) (b) \(1 : 2\) (c) \(2 : 1\) (d) \(5 : 1\)
  2. The ratio of 1 \(l\) to 50 \(ml\) is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    (a) \(1 : 5\) (b) \(1 : 20\) (c) \(20 : 1\) (d) \(5 : 1\)
  3. The length and breadth of a window are in 1m and 70 cm respectively. The ratio of the length to the breadth is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    (a) \(1 : 7\) (b) \(7 : 1\) (c) \(7 : 10\) (d) \(10 : 7\)
  4. The ratio of the number of sides of a triangle to the number of sides of a rectangle is
    (a) \(4 : 3\) (b) \(3 : 4\) (c) \(3 : 5\) (d) \(3 : 2\)
  5. If Azhagan is 50 years old and his son is 10 years old then the simplest ratio between the age of Azhagan to his son is
    (a) \(10 : 50\) (b) \(50 : 10\) (c) \(5 : 1\) (d) \(1 : 5\)
