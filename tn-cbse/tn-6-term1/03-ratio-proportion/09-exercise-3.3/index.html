Example 3.8

Pari wants to buy 5 tennis balls from a sports shop. If a dozen balls cost `180, how much should Pari pay to buy 5 balls?

Solution

By unitary method, we can solve this as follows :

Cost of a dozen balls = `180

Cost of 12 balls = `180 Cost of 1 ball \(= \frac{180}{12} =\) `15 Cost of 5 balls \(= 5 x 15 =\) `75 Hence, Pari has to pay `75 for 5 balls.

Example 3.9

A heater uses 3 units of electricity in 40 minutes. How many units does it consume in 2 hours?

Solution

In 40 minutes, electricity used \(= 3\) units. In 1 minute, electricity used \(= \frac{3}{40}\) units. In 120 minutes (2 hours), electricity used \(= \frac{3}{40}\) # \(120 = 9\) units Thus, the heater consumed 9 units of electricity in 2 hours.
