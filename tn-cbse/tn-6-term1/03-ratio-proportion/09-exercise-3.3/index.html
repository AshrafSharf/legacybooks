Exercise 3.3

Textbook pages 13–14 · 2 figures

  1. Fill in the boxes.
    1. \(3 : 5 :: \Box : 20\)
    2. \(\Box : 24 :: 3 : 8\)
    3. \(5 : \Box :: 10 : 8 :: 15 : \Box\)
    4. \(12 : \Box = \Box : 4 = 8 : 16\)
  2. Say True or False.
    1. 7 Persons is to 49 Persons as 11 kg is to 88 kg
    2. 10 books is to 15 books as 3 books is to 15 books
    3. If the weight of 40 books is 8 kg, then the weight of 15 books is 3 kg.
    4. A car travels 90 km in 3 hours with constant speed. It will travel 140 km in 5 hours at the same speed.
  3. Fill in the blanks.
    1. If the cost of 3 pens is ₹18, then the cost of 5 pens is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\) .
    2. If Karkuzhali earns ₹1800 in 15 days, then she earns ₹3000 in \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\) days.
  4. Find whether 12, 24, 18, 36 are in order that can be expressed as two ratios that are in proportion.
  5. Write the mean and extreme terms in the following ratios and check whether they are in proportion.
    1. 78 litre is to 130 litre and 12 bottles is to 20 bottles
    2. 400 gm is to 50 gm and 25 rupees is to 625 rupees
  1. The America's famous Golden Gate bridge is 6480 ft long with 756 ft tall towers. A model of this bridge exhibited in a fair is 60 ft long with 7 ft tall towers. Is the model, in proportion to the original bridge?
  2. If a person reads 20 pages of a book in 2 hours, how many pages will he read in 8 hours at the same speed?
  3. Cholan walks 6 km in 1 hour at constant speed. Find the distance covered by him in 20 minutes at the same speed.
  4. The number of correct answers given by Kaarmugilan and Kavitha in a quiz competition are in the ratio 10 : 11. If they had scored a total of 84 points in the competition, then how many points did Kavitha get?
  5. Karmegan made 54 runs in 9 overs and Asif made 77 runs in 11 overs. Whose run rate is better? (run rate = ratio of runs to overs)
  6. You purchase 6 apples for ₹90 and your friend purchases 5 apples for ₹70. Whose purchase is better?

Objective Type Questions

  1. Which of the following ratios are in proportion?
    (a) 3 : 5 , 6 : 11 (b) 2 : 3, 9 : 6 (c) 2 : 5, 10 : 25 (d) 3 : 1, 1 : 3
  2. If the ratios formed using the numbers 2, 5, \(x\), 20 in the same order are in proportion, then '\(x\)' is
    (a) 50 (b) 4 (c) 10 (d) 8
  3. If 7 : 5 is in proportion to \(x\) : 25, then '\(x\)' is
    (a) 27 (b) 49 (c) 35 (d) 14
  4. If a wooden doll costs ₹90, then the cost of 3 such dolls is ₹ \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    (a) 260 (b) 270 (c) 30 (d) 93
  5. If a man walks 2 km in 15 minutes, then he will walk \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\) km in 45 minutes.
    (a) 10 (b) 8 (c) 6 (d) 12
