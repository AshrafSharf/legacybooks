Challenging Problems

Textbook pages 29–30 · 2 figures

  1. Read the table and answer the following questions.
  1. Write the Canopus star's diameter in words, in the Indian and the International System.
  2. Write the sum of the place values of 5 in Sirius star's diameter in the Indian System.
  3. Eight hundred sixty four million seven hundred thirty. Write in Indian System.
  4. Write the diameter in words of Arcturus star in the International System.
  5. Write the difference of the diameters of Canopus and Arcturus stars in the Indian and the International Systems.
  6. Anbu asks Arivu Selvi to guess a five digit odd number. He gives the following hints.
  7. The digit in the 1000s place is less than 5
  8. The digit in the 100s place is greater than 6
  9. The digit in the 10s place is 8 What is Arivu Selvi answer? Does she give more than one answer?
  10. A Music concert is taking place in a stadium. A total of 7,689 chairs are to be put in rows of 90. (i) How many rows will there be? (ii) Will there be any chairs left over?
  11. Round off the seven digit number 29,75,842 to the nearest lakhs and ten lakhs. Are they the same?
  12. Find the 5 or 6 or 7 digit numbers from a newspaper or a magazine to get a rounded number to the nearest ten thousand.
