Chapter 3 · Algebra

Exercise 3.17

Textbook pages 60

  1. In the matrix \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 8 & 9 & 4 & 3 \\ -1 & \sqrt{7} & \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} & 5 \\ 1 & 4 & 3 & 0 \\ 6 & 8 & -11 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\), write
    1. The number of elements
    2. The order of the matrix
    3. Write the elements \(a_{22}, a_{23}, a_{24}, a_{34}, a_{43}, a_{44}\).
  2. If a matrix has 18 elements, what are the possible orders it can have? What if it has 6 elements?
  3. Construct a \(3 \times 3\) matrix whose elements are given by
    1. \(a_{ij} = |i - 2j|\)
    2. \(a_{ij} = \dfrac{(i + j)^3}{3}\)
  4. If \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 4 & 3 \\ 1 & -7 & 9 \\ 3 & 8 & 2 \end{pmatrix}\) then find the transpose of \(A\).
  5. If \(A = \begin{pmatrix} \sqrt{7} & -3 \\ -\sqrt{5} & 2 \\ \sqrt{3} & -5 \end{pmatrix}\) then find the transpose of \(-A\).
  6. If \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 2 & 2 \\ -\sqrt{17} & 0.7 & \frac{5}{2} \\ 8 & 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\) then verify \((A^T)^T = A\)
  7. Find the values of \(x,\ y\) and \(z\) from the following equations
    1. \(\begin{pmatrix} 12 & 3 \\ x & 5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} y & z \\ 3 & 5 \end{pmatrix}\)
    2. \(\begin{pmatrix} x + y & 2 \\ 5 + z & xy \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & 2 \\ 5 & 8 \end{pmatrix}\)
    3. \(\begin{pmatrix} x + y + z \\ x + z \\ y + z \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 9 \\ 5 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix}\)
