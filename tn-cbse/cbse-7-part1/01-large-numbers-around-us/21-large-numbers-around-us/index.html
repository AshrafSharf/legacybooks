Page No. 3 Figure it out

  • 1.According to the 2011 Census, the population of the town of

Chintamani was about 75,000. How much less than one lakh is 75,000?

Ans: 75,000 is 25,000 less than 1 lakh.

  • 2.The estimated population of Chintamani in the year 2024 is

1,06,000. How much more than one lakh is 1,06,000?

Ans: 1,06,000 is 6,000 more than 1 lakh.

  • 3.By how much did the population of Chintamani increase from

2011 to 2024?

Ans: Increase in population from 2011 to 2024 is 31,000.
