Chapter 1 · Large Numbers Around Us

Figure it Out

Textbook pages 9 · 1 figure

  1. Read the following numbers in Indian place value notation and write their number names in both the Indian and American systems:
    1. 4050678
    2. 48121620
    3. 20022002
    4. 246813579
    5. 345000543
    6. 1020304050
  2. Write the following numbers in Indian place value notation:
    1. One crore one lakh one thousand ten
    2. One billion one million one thousand one
    3. Ten crore twenty lakh thirty thousand forty
    4. Nine billion eighty million seven hundred thousand six hundred
  3. Compare and write ‘<’, ‘>’ or ‘=’:
    1. 30 thousand ____ 3 lakhs
    2. 500 lakhs ____ 5 million
    3. 800 thousand ____ 8 million
    4. 640 crore ____ 60 billion

We shall come across even bigger numbers in later grades.
