  1. 1.Using all digits from 0–9 exactly once (the first digit cannot be 0) to create a 10-digit number, write the —
    1. (a)Largest multiple of 5
    2. (b)Smallest even number
  2. 2.The number 10,30,285 in words is Ten lakhs thirty thousand two hundred eighty five, which has 42 letters. Give a 7-digit number name which has the maximum number of letters.
  3. 3.Write a 9-digit number where exchanging any two digits results in a bigger number. How many such numbers exist?
  4. 4.Strike out 10 digits from the number 12345123451234512345 so that the remaining number is as large as possible.
  5. 5.The words ‘zero’ and ‘one’ share letters ‘e’ and ‘o’. The words ‘one’ and ‘two’ share a letter ‘o’, and the words ‘two’ and ‘three’ also share a letter ‘t’. How far do you have to count to find two consecutive numbers which do not share an English letter in common?
  6. 6.Suppose you write down all the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, …, 9, 10, 11, … The tenth digit you write is ‘1’ and the eleventh digit is ‘0’, as part of the number 10.
    1. (a)What would the 1000th digit be? At which number would it occur?
    2. (b)What number would contain the millionth digit?
    3. (c)When would you have written the digit ‘5’ for the 5000th time?
  7. 7.A calculator has only ‘+10,000’ and ‘+100’ buttons. Write an expression describing the number of button clicks to be made for the following numbers:
    1. (a)20,800
    2. (b)92,100
    3. (c)1,20,500
    4. (d)65,30,000
    5. (e)70,25,700
  8. 8.How many lakhs make a billion?

  2. 11.Find out how many coins should be stacked to match the height of the Statue of Unity. Assume each coin is 1 mm thick.
  3. 12.Grey-headed albatrosses have a roughly 7-feet wide wingspan. They are known to migrate across several oceans. Albatrosses can cover about 900–1000 km in a day. One of the longest single trips recorded is about 12,000 km. How many days would such a trip take to cross the Pacific Ocean approximately?
  4. 13.A bar-tailed godwit holds the record for the longest recorded non-stop flight. It travelled 13,560 km from Alaska to Australia without stopping. Its journey started on 13 October 2022 and continued for about 11 days. Find out the approximate distance it covered every day. Find out the approximate distance it covered every hour.
  5. 14.Bald eagles are known to fly as high as 4500–6000 m above the ground level. Mount Everest is about 8850 m high. Aeroplanes can fly as high as 10,000–12,800 m. How many times bigger are these heights compared to Somu’s building?
