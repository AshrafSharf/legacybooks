Small Squares in Grids

Textbook pages 5–6 · 4 figures

In \(a 3 \times 3\) grid, there are 9 small squares, which is an odd number. Meanwhile, in \(a 3 \times 4\) grid, there are 12 small squares, which is an even number.

Given the dimensions of a grid, can you tell the parity of the number of small squares without calculating the product?

Find the parity of the number of small squares in these grids:

  • (a)\(27 \times 13\)
  • (b)\(42 \times 78\)
  • (c)\(135 \times 654\)
