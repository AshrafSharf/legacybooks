  1. Fill in the blanks with numbers and boxes by signs, so that the expressions on both sides are equal.
    1. \(3 \times (6 + 7) = 3 \times 6 + 3 \times 7\)
    2. \((8 + 3) \times 4 = 8 \times 4 + 3 \times 4\)
    3. \(3 \times (5 + 8) = 3 \times 5 \; \boxed{\phantom{+}} \; 3 \times \underline{\qquad}\)
    4. \((9 + 2) \times 4 = 9 \times 4 \; \boxed{\phantom{+}} \; 2 \times \underline{\qquad}\)
    5. \(3 \times (\underline{\qquad} + 4) = 3 \, \underline{\qquad} + \underline{\qquad}\)
    6. \((\underline{\qquad} + 6) \times 4 = 13 \times 4 + \underline{\qquad}\)
    7. \(3 \times (\underline{\qquad} + \underline{\qquad}) = 3 \times 5 + 3 \times 2\)
    8. \((\underline{\qquad} + \underline{\qquad}) \times \underline{\qquad} = 2 \times 4 + 3 \times 4\)
    9. \(5 \times (9 - 2) = 5 \times 9 - 5 \times \underline{\qquad}\)
    10. \((5 - 2) \times 7 = 5 \times 7 - 2 \times \underline{\qquad}\)
    11. \(5 \times (8 - 3) = 5 \times 8 \; \boxed{\phantom{+}} \; 5 \times \underline{\qquad}\)
    12. \((8 - 3) \times 7 = 8 \times 7 \; \boxed{\phantom{+}} \; 3 \times 7\)
    13. \(5 \times (12 - \underline{\qquad}) = \underline{\qquad} \; \boxed{\phantom{+}} \; 5 \times \underline{\qquad}\)
    14. \((15 - \underline{\qquad}) \times 7 = \underline{\qquad} \; \boxed{\phantom{+}} \; 6 \times 7\)
    15. \(5 \times (\underline{\qquad} - \underline{\qquad}) = 5 \times 9 - 5 \times 4\)
    16. \((\underline{\qquad} - \underline{\qquad}) \times \underline{\qquad} = 17 \times 7 - 9 \times 7\)

    Ans:

    (c) \(3 \times (5 + 8) = 3 \times 5 \; \boxed{+} \; 3 \times \underline{8}\)

    (d) \((9 + 2) \times 4 = 9 \times 4 \; \boxed{+} \; 2 \times 4\)

    (e) \(3 \times (\underline{10} + 4) = 3 \times \underline{10} + \underline{3 \times 4}\)

    (f) \((\underline{13} + 6) \times 4 = 13 \times 4 + 6 \times \underline{4}\)

    (g) \(3 \times (\underline{5} + \underline{2}) = 3 \times 5 + 3 \times 2\)

    (h) \((\underline{2} + \underline{3}) \times \underline{4} = 2 \times 4 + 3 \times 4\)

    (i) \(5 \times (9 - 2) = 5 \times 9 - 5 \times \underline{2}\)

    (j) \((5 - 2) \times 7 = 5 \times 7 - 2 \times \underline{7}\)

    (k) \(5 \times (8 - 3) = 5 \times 8 \; \boxed{-} \; 5 \times 3\)

    (l) \((8 - 3) \times 7 = 8 \times 7 \; \boxed{-} \; 3 \times 7\)

    (m) \(5 \times (12 - \underline{3}) = 5 \times \underline{12} \; \boxed{-} \; 5 \times \underline{3}\)

    (n) \((15 - \underline{6}) \times 7 = \underline{15 \times 7} \; \boxed{-} \; 6 \times 7\)

    (o) \(5 \times (\underline{9} - \underline{4}) = 5 \times 9 - 5 \times 4\)

    (p) \((\underline{17} - \underline{9}) \times \underline{7} = 17 \times 7 - 9 \times 7\)

    [In (e), (m) you may put any number other than 10 and 3 respectively.]

    (a) \(>\) : since \(8 - 3 > 3 - 8\)    (b) \(<\)    (c) \(<\)    (d) \(=\)
