  • 1.A water tank is filled from a tap. If the tap is open for 1 hour, \(\frac{𝟕}{𝟏𝟎}\) of the

tank gets filled. How much of the tank is filled if the tap is open for

  • (a)\(\frac{𝟏}{𝟑}\) hour ____________

Ans: \(\frac{7}{30}\) part

  • (b)\(\frac{𝟐}{𝟑}\) hour ____________

Ans: \(\frac{14}{30}\) part

  • (c)\(\frac{𝟑}{𝟒}\) hour ____________

Ans: \(\frac{21}{40}\) part

  • (d)\(\frac{𝟕}{𝟏𝟎}\) hour ____________

Ans: \(\frac{49}{100}\) part

  • (e)For the tank to be full, how long should the tap be running?

Ans: \(\frac{10}{7}\) hours

After giving them their shares, she keeps the remaining land for herself.

  • (a)What part of the original land did Krishna get?
  • (b)What part of the original land did Bora get?
  • (c)What part of the original land did Somu keep for herself?

Ans: Part of land that remains with somu \(= \frac{5}{6}\)

In a day, the number of times -

  • (a)the first fountain will fill the cistern is \(1 \div 1 = 1\)
  • (b)the second fountain will fill the cistern is \(1 \div \frac{𝟏}{𝟐} =\) _____

Ans: 2 times

  • (c)the third fountain will fill the cistern is \(1 \div \frac{𝟏}{𝟒} =\) _____

Ans: 4 times

  • (d)the fourth fountain will fill the cistern is \(1 \div \frac{𝟏}{𝟓} =\) _____

Ans: 5 times

The number of times the four fountains together will fill the cistern in

a day is ___ + ____ + ___ + ____ \(= 12\).

Ans: The number of times the four fountains together will fill the cistern in a day is

\(= 1 + 2 + 4 + 5 = 12\)
