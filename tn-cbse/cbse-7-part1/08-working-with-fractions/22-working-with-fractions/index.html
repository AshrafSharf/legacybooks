Figure it Out (Page 176)

  1. Tenzin drinks \(\frac{1}{2}\) glass of milk every day. How many glasses of milk does he drink in a week? How many glasses of milk did he drink in the month of January?

    Ans: Quantity of milk that Tenzin drinks in a week \(= \frac{7}{2}\) glasses. Quantity of milk Tenzin drinks in the month of January which has 31 days \(= \frac{31}{2}\) glasses.

  2. A team of workers can make 1 km of a water canal in 8 days. So, in one day, the team can make ____ km of the water canal. If they work 5 days a week, they can make ____ km of the water canal in a week.

    Ans: Length of water canal made in 1 day \(= \frac{1}{8}\) km. Length of water canal made in 5 days \(= \frac{5}{8}\) km.

  3. Manju and two of her neighbours buy 5 litres of oil every week and share it equally among the 3 families. How much oil does each family get in a week? How much oil will one family get in 4 weeks?

    Ans: Oil each family gets in 1 week \(= \frac{5}{3}\) litres. Oil each family gets in 4 weeks \(= \frac{20}{3}\) litres.

  4. Safia saw the Moon setting on Monday at 10 pm. Her mother, who is a scientist, told her that every day the Moon sets \(\frac{5}{6}\) hour later than the previous day. How many hours after 10 pm will the moon set on Thursday?

    Ans: Time at which the moon sets on Thursday (i.e. 3 days after Monday)

    \(= 10 + 2 + \frac{1}{2} = 12\frac{1}{2}\) am Or 12:30 am

Figure it Out (Page 177)

  1. Multiply and then convert it into a mixed fraction:
    1. \(7 \times \frac{3}{5}\) — Ans: \(7 \times \frac{3}{5} = \frac{21}{5} = 4\frac{1}{5}\)
    2. \(4 \times \frac{1}{3}\) — Ans: \(4 \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3} = 1\frac{1}{3}\)
    3. \(\frac{9}{7} \times 6\) — Ans: \(\frac{9}{7} \times 6 = \frac{54}{7} = 7\frac{5}{7}\)
    4. \(\frac{13}{11} \times 6\) — Ans: \(\frac{13}{11} \times 6 = \frac{78}{11} = 7\frac{1}{11}\)
