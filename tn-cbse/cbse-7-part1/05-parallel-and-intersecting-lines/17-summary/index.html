Chapter 5 · Parallel and Intersecting Lines

SUMMARY

Textbook pages 21–22

  1. When two lines intersect, they form four angles. The vertically opposite angles are equal and the linear pairs add up to 180°.
  2. When two lines intersect and the angles formed are 90° (i.e., all four angles are equal), the lines are said to be perpendicular to each other.
  3. When two lines never intersect on a plane, they are called parallel lines.
  4. When a line t intersects another pair of lines, it is called a transversal and it forms 2 sets of 4 angles. Each of the 4 angles in the first set has a corresponding angle in the second set.
  5. When a transversal intersects a pair of parallel lines, the corresponding angles are equal. When a transversal intersects a pair of lines and the corresponding angles are equal, then the pair of lines is parallel.
  6. When a transversal intersects a pair of parallel lines, the alternate angles are equal.
  7. The interior angles on the same side formed by a transversal intersecting a pair of parallel lines always add up to 180°.
