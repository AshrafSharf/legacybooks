Page No. 84 — Figure it Out

  1. Write formulas for the perimeter of: (a) triangle with all sides equal (b) a regular pentagon (c) a regular hexagon

    Ans:

    1. Let length of each side of the triangle \(= a\) units. Perimeter of Triangle \(= 3a\) units
    2. Let length of each side of pentagon \(= a\) units. Perimeter of Regular Pentagon \(= 5a\) units
    3. Let length of each side of regular hexagon \(= a\) units. Perimeter of Regular Hexagon \(= 6a\) units
  2. Munirathna has a 20 m long pipe. However, he wants a longer watering pipe for his garden. He joins another pipe of some length to this one. Give the expression for the combined length of the pipe. Use the letter-number 'k' to denote the length in meters of the other pipe.

    Ans: \((20 + k)\) meters
