Rupee ─ Paise conversion

Textbook pages 23–24 · 3 figures

You may have heard of ‘paisa’. 100 paise is equal to 1 rupee. As we have coins and notes for rupees, coins for paise were also used commonly until recently. There were coins for 1 paisa, 2 paise, 3 paise, 5 paise, 10 paise, 20 paise, 25 paise, and 50 paise. All denominations of 25 paise and less were removed from use in the year 2011. But we still see paise in bills, account statements, etc. 1 rupee \(= 100\) paise

1 paisa \(= \frac{1}{100}\) rupee \(= 0.01\) rupee As each paisa is one-hundredth of a rupee,

75 paise \(= \frac{75}{100}\) rupee

= ( \(\frac{70}{100} + 5100)\) rupee = ( \(\frac{7}{10} + 5100)\) rupee \(= 0.75\) rupee.
