Chapter 3 · A Peek Beyond the Point

SUMMARY

Textbook pages 35–36 · 4 figures

  1. We can split a unit into smaller parts to get more exact/accurate measurements.
  2. We extended the Indian place value system and saw that » 1 unit \(= 10\) one-tenths, » 1 tenth \(= 10\) one-hundredths, » 1 hundredth \(= 10\) one-thousandths, » 10 one-hundredths \(= 1\) tenth, » 100 one-hundredths \(= 1\) unit.
  3. A decimal point (‘.’) is used in the Indian place value system to separate the whole number part of a number from its fractional part.
  4. We also learnt how to compare decimal numbers, locate them on the number line, and perform addition and subtraction on them.
