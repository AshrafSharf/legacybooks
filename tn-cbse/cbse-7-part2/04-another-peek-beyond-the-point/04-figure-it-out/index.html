Figure it Out

Textbook pages 7–8 · 5 figures

  1. Recall that a tenth is 0.1, a hundredth is 0.01, and so on. Find the following products in tenths, hundredths and so on:
    1. \(6 \times 4\) tenths \(= 24\) tenths
    2. \(7 \times 0.3\)
    3. \(9 \times 5\) hundredths
  2. Find the products:
    1. \(27.34 \times 6\)
    2. \(4.23 \times 3.7\)
    3. \(0.432 \times 0.23\)
  3. Thejus needs 1.65 m of cloth for a shirt. How many metres of cloth are needed for 3 shirts?
  4. Meenu bought 4 notebooks and 3 erasers. The cost of each book was ₹15.50 and each eraser was ₹2.75. How much did she spend in all?
  5. The thickness of a rupee coin is 1.45 mm. What is the total height of the cylinder formed by placing 36 rupee coins one over the other? Write the answer in centimeters.
  1. The price of 1 kg of oranges is ₹56.50. What is the price of 2.250 kg of oranges? Can we write 56.50 as 56.5 and 2.250 as 2.25 and multiply? Will we get the same product? Why?
  2. Dwarakanath purchases notebooks at a wholesale price of ₹23.6 per piece and sells each notebook at ₹30/-. How much profit does he make if he sells 50 books in a week?
  3. Given that \(18 \times 12 = 216\), find the products:
    1. \(18 \times 1.2\)
    2. \(18 \times 0.12\)
    3. \(1.8 \times 1.2\)
    4. \(0.18 \times 0.12\)
    5. \(0.018 \times 0.012\)
    6. \(1.8 \times 12\)

    In which of the cases above is the product less than 1?

  4. In which of the following multiplications is the product less than 1? Can you find the answer without actually doing the multiplications?
    1. \(7 \times 0.6\)
    2. \(0.7 \times 0.6\)
    3. \(0.7 \times 6\)
    4. \(0.07 \times 0.06\)
  5. Multiplying the following numbers by 10, 100 and 1000 to complete the table.
