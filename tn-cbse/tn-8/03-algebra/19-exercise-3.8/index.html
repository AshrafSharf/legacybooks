  1. Fill in the blanks:
    1. X- axis and Y-axis intersect at \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    2. The coordinates of the point in third quadrant are always \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\).
    3. \((0,-5)\) point lies on \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\) axis.
    4. The x- coordinate is always \(\underline{\qquad}\) on the y-axis.
    5. \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\) coordinates are the same for a line parallel to Y-axis.
  2. Say True or False:
    1. \((-10,20)\) lies in the second quadrant.
    2. \((-9,0)\) lies on the \(x\)-axis.
    3. The coordinates of the origin are \((1,1)\).
  3. Find the quadrants without plotting the points on a graph sheet.
    \((3,-4)\), \((5,7)\), \((2,0)\), \((-3,-5)\), \((4,-3)\), \((-7,2)\), \((-8,0)\), \((0,10)\), \((-9,50)\).
  4. Plot the following points in a graph sheet.
    A\((5,2)\), B\((-7,-3)\), C\((-2,4)\), D\((-1,-1)\), E\((0,-5)\), F\((2,0)\), G\((7,-4)\), H\((-4,0)\), I\((2,3)\), J\((8,-4)\), K\((0,7)\).
