Exercise 3.1

Textbook pages 8–9

  1. Complete the table.
    \(\times\)\(2x^{2}\)\(-2xy\)\(x^{4}y^{3}\)\(2xyz\)\((\underline{\quad})xz^{2}\)
    \(x^{4}\)
    \((\underline{\qquad})\)\(4x^{5}y^{4}\)
    \(-x^{2}y\)
    \(2y^{2}z\)\(-10xy^{2}z^{3}\)
    \(-3xyz\)
    \((\underline{\qquad})\)\(-14xyz^{2}\)
  2. Find the product of the terms.
    1. \(-2mn, (2m)^{2}, -3mn\)
    2. \(3x^{2}y, -3xy^{3}, x^{2}y^{2}\)
  3. If \(l = 4pq^{2}\), \(b = -3p^{2}q\), \(h = 2p^{3}q^{3}\) then, find the value of \(l \times b \times h\).
  4. Expand
    1. \(5x(2y - 3)\)
    2. \(-2p(5p^{2} - 3p + 7)\)
    3. \(3mn(m^{3}n^{3} - 5m^{2}n + 7mn^{2})\)
    4. \(x^{2}(x + y + z) + y^{2}(x + y + z) + z^{2}(x - y - z)\)
  5. Find the product of
    1. \((2x + 3)(2x - 4)\)
    2. \((y^{2} - 4)(2y^{2} + 3y)\)
    3. \((m^{2} - n)(5m^{2}n^{2} - n^{2})\)
    4. \(3(x - 5) \times 2(x - 1)\)
  6. Find the missing term.
    1. \(6xy \times \underline{\qquad\qquad} = -12x^{3}y\)
    2. \(\underline{\qquad\qquad} \times (-15m^{2}n^{3}p) = 45m^{3}n^{3}p^{2}\)
    3. \(2y(5x^{2}y - \underline{\qquad} + 3\underline{\qquad}) = 10x^{2}y^{2} - 2xy + 6y^{3}\)
  7. Match the following.
    a)\(4y^{2} \times -3y\)(i)\(20x^{2}y - 20x\)
    b)\(-2xy(5x^{2} - 3)\)(ii)\(5x^{3} - 5xy^{2} + 5x^{2}y\)
    c)\(5x(x^{2} - y^{2} + xy)\)(iii)\(4x^{2} - 9\)
    d)\((2x + 3)(2x - 3)\)(iv)\(-12y^{3}\)
    e)\(5x(4xy - 4)\)(v)\(-10x^{3}y + 6xy\)

    A) iv, v, ii, i, iii    B) v, iv, iii, ii, i    C) iv, v, ii, iii, i    D) iv, v, iii, ii, i

  8. A car moves at a uniform speed of \((x + 30)\) km/hr. Find the distance covered by the car in \((y + 2)\) hours. (Hint: distance \(=\) speed \(\times\) time).

Objective Type Questions

  1. The product of \(7p^{3}\) and \((2p^{2})^{2}\) is
    1. \(14p^{12}\)
    2. \(28p^{7}\)
    3. \(9p^{7}\)
    4. \(11p^{12}\)
  2. The missing terms in the product \(-3m^{3}n \times 9(\underline{\quad}) = \underline{\qquad\qquad}\,m^{4}n^{3}\) are
    1. \(mn^{2}, 27\)
    2. \(m^{2}n, 27\)
    3. \(m^{2}n^{2}, -27\)
    4. \(mn^{2}, -27\)
  3. If the area of a square is \(36x^{4}y^{2}\) then, its side is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\)
    1. \(6x^{4}y^{2}\)
    2. \(8x^{2}y^{2}\)
    3. \(6x^{2}y\)
    4. \(-6x^{2}y\)
  4. If the area of a rectangle is \(48m^{2}n^{3}\) and whose length is \(8mn^{2}\) then, its breadth is \(\underline{\quad}\).
    1. \(6mn\)
    2. \(8m^{2}n\)
    3. \(7m^{2}n^{2}\)
    4. \(6m^{2}n^{2}\)
  5. If the area of a rectangular land is \((a^{2} - b^{2})\) sq.units whose breadth is \((a - b)\) then, its length is \(\underline{\qquad\qquad}\)
    1. \(a - b\)
    2. \(a + b\)
    3. \(a^{2} - b\)
    4. \((a + b)^{2}\)
