4.2 Applications of Percentage in Word Problems

Textbook pages 2–4 · 3 figures

We know that Per Cent means per hundred or out of a hundred. It is denoted by the symbol %. x% denotes the fraction 100x . It is very useful in comparing quantities easily. Let us see its uses in the following word problems.

Example 4.1

If x % of 600 is 450, then find the value of x.

Solution:

Given, x% of \(600 = 450\)

\(\frac{x}{100} x = \frac{450}{6}\)

\(\times 600 = 450\)

\(x = 75\)

Example 4.3

Akila scored 80% of marks in an examination. If her score was 576 marks, then find the maximum marks of the examination.

Solution:

Let the maximum marks be x .

Now, 80% of \(x = 576\)

\(\frac{80}{100} \times x = 576 \Rightarrow x = 576 \times \frac{100}{80} = 720\) marks

Therefore, the maximum marks of the examination \(= 720\).
