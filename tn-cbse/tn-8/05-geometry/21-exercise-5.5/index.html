I. Construct the following parallelograms with the given measurements and find their area.

  1. ARTS, AR=6cm, RT=5cm and \(\angle ART =70 ^{\circ}\) .
  2. CAMP, CA=6cm, AP=8cm and CP=5.5cm.
  3. EARN, ER=10cm, AN=7cm and \(\angle EOA=110 ^{\circ}\) where ER and AN intersect at O.
  4. GAIN, GA=7.5cm, GI=9cm and \(\angle GAI =100 ^{\circ}\) . II. Construct the following rhombuses with the given measurements and also find their area.
  5. FACE, \(FA= 6\) cm and \(FC = 8\) cm (iii) LUCK, \(LC = 7.8\) cm and \(UK = 6\) cm
  6. CAKE, CA=5 cm and \(\angle A = 65 ^{\circ}\) (iv) PARK, \(PR = 9\) cm and \(\angle P =70 ^{\circ}\) III. Construct the following rectangles with the given measurements and also find their area.
  7. HAND, \(HA = 7\) cm and \(AN = 4\) cm (ii) LAND, \(LA = 8\) cm and \(AD = 10\) cm IV. Construct the following squares with the given measurements and also find their area.
  8. EAST, \(EA = 6.5\) cm (ii) WEST, \(WS = 7.5\) cm
