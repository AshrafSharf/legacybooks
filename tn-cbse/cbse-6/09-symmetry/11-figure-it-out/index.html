  • 3.Draw, wherever possible, a rough sketch of:

symmetry.

  • 6.Can we have a figure with rotational symmetry whose smallest

angle of symmetry is:

  • a.\(45 ^{\circ}\) ?
  • b.\(17 ^{\circ}\) ?

the angles of rotational symmetry.
