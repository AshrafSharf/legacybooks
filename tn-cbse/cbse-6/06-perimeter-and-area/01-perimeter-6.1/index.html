6.1 Perimeter

Textbook pages 1 · 2 figures

Do you remember what the perimeter of a closed plane figure is? Let us refresh our understanding! The perimeter of any closed plane figure is the distance covered along its boundary when you go around it once. For a polygon, i.e., a closed plane figure made up of line segments, the perimeter is simply the sum of the lengths of its all sides, i.e., the total distance along its outer boundary.
