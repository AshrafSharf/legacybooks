Like general fractions, general integers (including zero and the negative numbers) were first conceived of and used in Asia, thousands of years ago, before they eventually spread across the world in more modern times. The first known instances of the use of negative numbers occurred in the context of accounting. In one of China’s most important mathematical works, The Nine Chapters on Mathematical Art (Jiuzhang Suanshu) - which was completed by the first or second century \(CE -\) positive and negative numbers were represented using red and black rods, much like the way we represented them using green and red tokens!
