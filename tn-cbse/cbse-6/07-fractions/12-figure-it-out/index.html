Writing fractions greater than one as mixed numbers

We saw that: \(\frac{3}{2} = 1 + \frac{1}{2}\) .

We can write other fractions in a similar way. For example,

\(\frac{4}{3} = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} = 1 + \frac{1}{3}\) .

\(3 \times \frac{1}{3} = 1\)
