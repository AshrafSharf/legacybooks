Figure it Out

Textbook pages 24–25

  1. Compare the following fractions and justify your answers:
    1. \(\frac{8}{3}, \frac{5}{2}\)
    2. \(\frac{4}{9}, \frac{3}{7}\)
    3. \(\frac{7}{10}, \frac{9}{14}\)
    4. \(\frac{12}{5}, \frac{8}{5}\)
    5. \(\frac{9}{4}, \frac{5}{2}\)
  2. Write the following fractions in ascending order.
    1. \(\frac{7}{10}, \frac{11}{15}, \frac{2}{5}\)
    2. \(\frac{19}{24}, \frac{5}{6}, \frac{7}{12}\)
  3. Write the following fractions in descending order.
    1. \(\frac{25}{16}, \frac{7}{8}, \frac{13}{4}, \frac{17}{32}\)
    2. \(\frac{3}{4}, \frac{12}{5}, \frac{7}{12}, \frac{5}{4}\)
