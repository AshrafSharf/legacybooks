Figure it Out

Textbook pages 32

  1. Carry out the following subtractions using Brahmagupta’s method:
    1. \(\frac{8}{15} - \frac{3}{15}\)
    2. \(\frac{2}{5} - \frac{4}{15}\)
    3. \(\frac{5}{6} - \frac{4}{9}\)
    4. \(\frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{2}\)
  2. Subtract as indicated:
    1. \(\frac{13}{4}\) from \(\frac{10}{3}\)
    2. \(\frac{18}{5}\) from \(\frac{23}{3}\)
    3. \(\frac{29}{7}\) from \(\frac{45}{7}\)
  3. Solve the following problems:
    1. Jaya’s school is \(\frac{7}{10}\) km from her home. She takes an auto for \(\frac{1}{2}\) km from her home daily, and then walks the remaining distance to reach her school. How much does she walk daily to reach the school?
    2. Jeevika takes \(\frac{10}{3}\) minutes to take a complete round of the park and her friend Namit takes \(\frac{13}{4}\) minutes to do the same. Who takes less time and by how much?
