2.2 Line Segment

Textbook pages 2 · 3 figures

Fold a piece of paper and unfold it. Do you see a crease? This gives the idea of a line segment. It has two end points, A and B. Mark any two points A and B on a sheet of paper. Try to connect A to B by various routes (Fig. 2.1). What is the shortest route from A to B? This shortest path from point A to Point B (including A and B) as shown here is called the line segment from A to B. It is denoted by either AB or BA. The points A and B are called

the end points of the line segment AB.
