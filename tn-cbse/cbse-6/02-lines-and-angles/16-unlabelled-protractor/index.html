Unlabelled protractor

Textbook pages 23 · 2 figures

Here is a protractor. Do you see the straight angle at the center divided into 180 units of 1 degree? Only part of the lines dividing the straight angle are visible, though! Starting from the marking on the rightmost point of the base, there is a long mark for every \(10 ^{\circ}\) . From every such long mark, there is a medium sized mark after \(5 ^{\circ}\) .
