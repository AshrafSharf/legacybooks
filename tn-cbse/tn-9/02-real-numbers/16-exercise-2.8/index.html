Exercise 2.8

  1. Represent the following numbers in the scientific notation:

    1. 569430000000
    2. 2000.57
    3. 0.0000006000
    4. 0.0009000002

  2. Write the following numbers in decimal form:

    1. \(3.459 \times 10^{6}\)
    2. \(5.678 \times 10^{4}\)
    3. \(1.00005 \times 10^{-5}\)
    4. \(2.530009 \times 10^{-7}\)

  3. Represent the following numbers in scientific notation:

    1. \(\left(300000\right)^{2} \times \left(20000\right)^{4}\)
    2. \(\left(0.000001\right)^{11} \div \left(0.005\right)^{3}\)
    3. \(\left\{\left(0.00003\right)^{6} \times \left(0.00005\right)^{4}\right\} \div \left\{\left(0.009\right)^{3} \times \left(0.05\right)^{2}\right\}\)

  4. Represent the following information in scientific notation:

    1. The world population is nearly 7,000,000,000.
    2. One light year means the distance 9,460,528,400,000,000 km.
    3. Mass of an electron is 0.000 000 000 000 000 000 000 000 000 000 91093822 kg.

  5. Simplify:

    1. \((2.75 \times 10^{7}) + (1.23 \times 10^{8})\)
    2. \((1.598 \times 10^{17}) - (4.58 \times 10^{15})\)
    3. \((1.02 \times 10^{10}) \times (1.20 \times 10^{-3})\)
    4. \((8.41 \times 10^{4}) \div (4.3 \times 10^{5})\)
