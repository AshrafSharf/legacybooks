Chapter 6 · Trigonometry

Exercise 6.3

Textbook pages 14

Find the value of the following:

  1. \(\left(\frac{\cos 47^\circ}{\sin 43^\circ}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sin 72^\circ}{\cos 18^\circ}\right)^2 - 2\cos^2 45^\circ\)
  2. \(\frac{\cos 70^\circ}{\sin 20^\circ} + \frac{\cos 59^\circ}{\sin 31^\circ} + \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin(90^\circ - \theta)} - 8\cos^2 60^\circ\)
  3. \(\tan 15^\circ \tan 30^\circ \tan 45^\circ \tan 60^\circ \tan 75^\circ\)
  4. \(\frac{\cot\theta}{\tan(90^\circ - \theta)} + \frac{\cos(90^\circ - \theta)\tan\theta\sec(90^\circ - \theta)}{\sin(90^\circ - \theta)\cot(90^\circ - \theta)\operatorname{cosec}(90^\circ - \theta)}\)

Thinking Corner

  1. What are the minimum and maximum values of \(\sin\theta\) ? \((0^\circ \le \theta \le 90^\circ)\)
  2. What are the minimum and maximum values of \(\cos\theta\) ? \((0^\circ \le \theta \le 90^\circ)\)
