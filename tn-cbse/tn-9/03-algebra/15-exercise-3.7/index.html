Exercise 3.7

Textbook pages 34–35 · 4 figures

  1. Find the quotient and remainder of the following.
    1. \((4x^{3} + 6x^{2} - 23x + 18) \div (x + 3)\)
    2. \((8y^{3} - 16y^{2} + 16y - 15) \div (2y - 1)\)
    3. \((8x^{3} - 1) \div (2x - 1)\)
    4. \((-18z + 14z^{2} + 24z^{3} + 18) \div (3z + 4)\)
  2. The area of a rectangle is \(x^{2} + 7x + 12\). If its breadth is \((x+3)\), then find its length.
  3. The base of a parallelogram is \((5x+4)\). Find its height, if the area is \(25x^{2} - 16\).
  4. The sum of \((x+5)\) observations is \((x^{3} + 125)\). Find the mean of the observations.
  5. Find the quotient and remainder for the following using synthetic division:
    1. \((x^{3} + x^{2} - 7x - 3) \div (x - 3)\)
    2. \((x^{3} + 2x^{2} - x - 4) \div (x + 2)\)
    3. \((8x^{4} - 2x^{2} + 6x + 5) \div (4x + 1)\)
  6. If the quotient obtained on dividing \((8x^{4} - 2x^{2} + 6x - 7)\) by \((2x + 1)\) is \((4x^{3} + px^{2} - qx + 3)\), then find p, q and also the remainder.
  7. If the quotient obtained on dividing \(3x^{3} + 11x^{2} + 34x + 106\) by \(x - 3\) is \(3x^{2} + ax + b\), then find a, b and also the remainder.
