    1. The cost of a notebook is twice the cost of a pen. Consider the cost of a notebook to be ₹\(t\) and that of a pen to be ₹\(p\). Charlie wrote \(t = 2p\), whereas Meera wrote \(p = 2t\). Which of these two representations is correct?
    2. In a one-day International Cricket match between India and Sri Lanka played in Nagpur, two Indian batsmen together scored 176 runs. Manisha expressed this situation as \(x + y = 176\), where the number of runs scored by one batsman is \(x\), and the number of runs scored by the other is \(y\). Is this a correct representation?
