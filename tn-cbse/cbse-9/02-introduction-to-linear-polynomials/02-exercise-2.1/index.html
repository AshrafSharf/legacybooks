Exercise 2.1

Textbook pages 3–4 · 4 figures

  1. Find the degrees of the following polynomials:
    1. \(2x^2 - 5x + 3\)
    2. \(y^3 + 2y - 1\)
    3. \(-9\)
    4. \(4z - 3\)
  2. Write polynomials of degrees 1, 2 and 3.
  3. What are the coefficients of \(x^2\) and \(x^3\) in the polynomial \(x^4 - 3x^3 + 6x^2 - 2x + 7\)?
  4. What is the coefficient of \(z^2\) in the polynomial \(4z^3 + 5z^2 - 11\)?
  5. What is the constant term of the polynomial \(9x^3 + 5x^2 - 8x - 10\)?

Recall that polynomials of degree 1 are called linear polynomials. In this chapter, we shall study linear polynomials.
