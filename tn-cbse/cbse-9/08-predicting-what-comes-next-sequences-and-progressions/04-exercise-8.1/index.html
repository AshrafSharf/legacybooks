Chapter 8 · Predicting What Comes Next Sequences and Progressions

Exercise 8.1

Textbook pages 6–7 · 1 figure

  1. Find the first five terms of the sequence in which the \(n^{\text{th}}\) term is given by (i) \(t_{n} = 3n - 4\), (ii) \(t_{n} = 2 - 5n\), and (iii) \(t_{n} = n^{2} - 2n + 3\) for \(n \ge 1\).
  2. Find the \(10^{\text{th}}\) and \(15^{\text{th}}\) terms of the sequence \(t_{n} = 5n - 3\) for \(n \ge 1\).
  3. Determine whether 97 and 172 are terms of the sequence \(t_{n} = 5n - 3\) for \(n \ge 1\).
  4. Which term of the sequence \(t_{n} = 5n - 3\) for \(n \ge 1\) is 607?
  5. A sequence is given by the recursive rule \(t_{1} = -5\), \(t_{n+1} = t_n + 3\) for \(n \ge 1\). Find the first five terms of the sequence. Is 52 a term of this sequence? If so, which term is it?
  6. Let \(T_{1} = 1\), \(T_{2} = 2\), \(T_{3} = 4\), and \(T_n = T_{n-1} + T_{n-2} + T_{n-3}\) for \(n \ge 4\). Find \(T_4\), \(T_5\), \(T_6\), \(T_7\), and \(T_8\).
