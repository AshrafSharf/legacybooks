  1. In a circle with centre O, the central angle AOB is \(60°\). If the radius of the circle is 12 cm, what is the length of the chord AB?
  2. Let A and B be two points on a circle with centre O.
    1. Are there points X, Y on the circle, on the same side of AB, such that \(\angle AXB\) is different from \(\angle AYB\)?
    2. Is it true that if \(\angle AXB = \angle AYB\), then X and Y lie on the same side of the circle?
    3. If \(\angle AXB = \angle AYB\), and X and Y do not lie on the circle, does the circle through A, B and X also pass through Y?
