Chapter opener

Textbook pages 1–2 · 5 figures

Humanity has always been fascinated by the shapes of the things around them. In some early cave paintings, the sun is depicted as a circle. In the cave paintings of Gudahandi in Odisha, one sees numerous geometric patterns including triangles, squares, circles, and ovals. These shapes were likely inspired by what humans saw in nature. Can you recognise the origin of the shapes in Fig. 5.1?

Circles form when raindrops fall on water. The cross-section of a plant stem and the inflorescence of a sunflower are also circular in shape. The full moon and sun also look circular.

What properties are common to all circles, big and small? You have studied one such property in Grade 7. Humans must have noticed it after observing many circular patterns in nature. Every circle has a centre. All points on the circle are at equal distance from the centre. We turn this observation around and make it the definition of a circle.

Activity: List some objects from nature that resemble a circle.
