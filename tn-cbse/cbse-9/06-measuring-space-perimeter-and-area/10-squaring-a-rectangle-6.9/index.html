6.9 Squaring a Rectangle

Textbook pages 23–25 · 5 figures

In ancient times, to ‘square a given shape’ meant to ‘construct a square equal in area to that shape’. The shape could be a rectangle or a triangle or a circle. Here, we show how the ancient Indian mathematician Baudhāyana squared a rectangle.
