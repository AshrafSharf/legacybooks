  1. Fill in the blanks to complete the following identities:
    1. \(s^2 - 11s + 24 = (\underline{\qquad})(\underline{\qquad})\)
    2. \((\underline{\qquad})(x + 1) = (3x^2 - 4x - 7)\)
    3. \(10x^2 - 11x - 6 = (2x - \underline{\qquad})(\underline{\qquad} + 2)\)
    4. \(6x^2 + 7x + 2 = (\underline{\qquad})(\underline{\qquad})\)
  2. Select and use the identity that will help you to find the following products without multiplying directly:
    1. \((41)^2\)
    2. \((27)^2\)
    3. \(23 \times 17\)
    4. \((135)^2\)
    5. \((97)^2\)
    6. \(18 \times 29\)
    7. \(34 \times 43\)

    1. \(9a^2 + b^2 + 4c^2 - 6ab + 12ac - 4bc\)
    2. \(16s^2 + 25t^2 - 40st\)
    3. \(r^2 - r - 42\)
    4. \(49g^2 + 14gh + h^2\)
    5. \(64u^2 + 121v^2 + 4w^2 - 176uv - 32uw + 44vw\)

Think and Reflect

James and Reshma were talking about algebraic identities they learnt in school.

James: \((a - b)^2(a + b) = (a^2 - 2ab + b^2)(a + b)\)

Reshma: I have a different idea. \((a - b)^2(a + b) = (a - b)[(a - b)(a + b)]\)

\(= (a - b)(a^2 - b^2)\)

I will find this product to get the answer.

According to you, who is correct and why? Try to combine more such identities and find new results.
