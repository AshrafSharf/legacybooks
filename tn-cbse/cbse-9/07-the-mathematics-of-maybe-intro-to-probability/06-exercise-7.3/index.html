Exercise 7.3

Textbook pages 13–14 · 2 figures

  1. When a single 6-sided die is rolled, what is the total number of possible outcomes in the sample space?
  2. For the following experiments write down the sample space S.
    1. Rolling a die and tossing a coin together.
    2. Choosing a random integer between \(-5\) and \(+5\).
    3. A box containing 5 green and 7 red balls. One ball is drawn at random.
  3. In a village fair, there are 3 popular snacks available: Samosa, Pakora, and Bhaji. For drinks, villagers can choose either Chai or Lassi.
    1. List the sample space of all possible snack and drink combinations a person could choose at the fair.
    2. List the event ‘Selecting Samosa as a snack.’
