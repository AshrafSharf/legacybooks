5.4.3 Construction of special angles without using protractor.

  1. Construction of angle of measure \(60 ^{\circ}\)

Step 1: Draw a line. Mark a point A on it. Step 2: With A as center draw an arc of A convenient radius to the line to meet at a point B.

Step 3: With the same radius and B as center draw an arc to cut the previous arc at C.

Step 4: Join AC. Then \(\angle BAC\) is the required angle with the measure \(60 ^{\circ}\) .

  1. Construction of angle of measure \(120 ^{\circ}\) .

We know that there are two \(60 ^{\circ}\) agnels in \(120 ^{\circ}\) . Hence, to construct \(120 ^{\circ}\) , we can construct two \(60 ^{\circ}\) angles consecutively as follows. Step 1: Draw a line. Mark a point A on it. Step 2: With A as center, draw an arc of convenient radius to the line at a point B. A

Step 3: With the same radius and B as center, draw an arc to cut the previous arc at C.

Step 4: With the same radius and C as center, draw an arc to cut the arc drawn in step 2 at D.

Step 5: Join AD. Then \(\angle BAD\) is the required angle with measure \(120 ^{\circ}\) .

  1. Construction of angle of measure \(30 ^{\circ}\)

Since \(30 ^{\circ}\) is half of \(60 ^{\circ}\) , we can construct \(30 ^{\circ}\) by bisecting the angle \(60 ^{\circ}\) . Step 1: Construct angle \(60 ^{\circ}\) [Refer Construction of angle of measure \(60 ^{\circ}\) (i)].

Step 2: With B as center, draw an arc of convenient radius in the interior of \(\angle BAC\).

Step 3: With the same radius and C as center, draw an arc to cut the previous arc at D.

Step 4: Join AD. Then \(\angle BAD\) is the required angle with measure \(30 ^{\circ}\) [Think about \(\angle\) DAC?]

  1. Construction of angle of measure \(90 ^{\circ}\)

Step 1: Construct angle \(120 ^{\circ}\) [Refer Construction of angle of measure \(120 ^{\circ}\) (ii)].

Step 2: With C as center, draw an arc of convenient radius in the interior of \(\angle CAD\).

Step 3: With the same radius and D as center, draw an arc to cut drawn in step 3 at E.

Step 4: Join AE. Then \(\angle BAD = 90 ^{\circ}\) is the required angle
