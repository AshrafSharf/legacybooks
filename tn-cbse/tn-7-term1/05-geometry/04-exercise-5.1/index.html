Exercise 5.1

Textbook pages 10–11 · 8 figures

  1. Given that \(AB\) is a straight line. Calculate the value of \(x^{\circ}\) in the following cases.
  2. One angle of a linear pair is a right angle. What can you say about the other angle?
  3. If the three angles at a point are in the ratio 1 : 4 : 7, find the value of each angle?
  4. There are six angles at a point. One of them is \(45^{\circ}\) and the other five angles are all equal. What is the measure of all the five angles?
  5. In the given figure, identify
    1. any two pairs of adjacent angles.
    2. two pairs of vertically opposite angles.
  6. The angles at a point are \(x^{\circ}\), \(2x^{\circ}\), \(3x^{\circ}\), \(4x^{\circ}\) and \(5x^{\circ}\). Find the value of the largest angle?
  7. From the given figure, find the missing angle.
  8. Find the angles \(x^{\circ}\) and \(y^{\circ}\) in the figure shown.
  9. Using the figure, answer the following questions.
    1. What is the measure of angle \(x^{\circ}\)?
    2. What is the measure of angle \(y^{\circ}\)?

Objective type questions

  1. Adjacent angles have
    1. no common interior, no common arm, no common vertex
    2. one common vertex, one common arm, common interior
    3. one common arm, one common vertex, no common interior
    4. one common arm, no common vertex, no common interior
  2. In the given figure the angles \(\angle 1\) and \(\angle 2\) are
    1. opposite angles
    2. Adjacent angles
    3. Linear pair
    4. Supplementary angles
  3. Vertically opposite angles are
    1. not equal in measure
    2. complementary
    3. supplementary
    4. equal in measure
  4. The sum of all angles at a point is
    1. \(360^{\circ}\)
    2. \(180^{\circ}\)
    3. \(90^{\circ}\)
    4. \(0^{\circ}\)
  5. The measure of \(\angle BOC\) is
    1. \(90^{\circ}\)
    2. \(180^{\circ}\)
    3. \(80^{\circ}\)
    4. \(100^{\circ}\)
