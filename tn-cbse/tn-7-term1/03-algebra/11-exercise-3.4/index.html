  1. Subtract \(-3ab - 8\) from \(3ab + 8\). Also, subtract \(3ab + 8\) from \(-3ab - 8\).
  2. Find the perimeter of a triangle whose sides are \(x + 3y\), \(2x + y\), \(x - y\).
  3. Thrice a number when increased by 5 gives 44. Find the number.
  4. How much smaller is \(2ab + 4b - c\) than \(5ab - 3b + 2c\)
  5. Six times a number subtracted from 40 gives \(-8\). Find the number.

Challenge Problems

  1. From the sum of \(5x + 7y - 12\) and \(3x - 5y + 2\), subtract the sum of \(2x - 7y - 1\) and \(-6x + 3y + 9\).
  2. Find the expression to be added with \(5a - 3b + 2c\) to get \(a - 4b - 2c\)?
  3. What should be subtracted from \(2m + 8n + 10\) to get \(-3m + 7n + 16\)?
  4. Give an algebraic equation for the following statement:
    “The difference between the area and perimeter of a rectangle is 20”
  5. Add: \(2a + b + 3c\) and \(a + \frac{1}{3}b + \frac{2}{5}c\)

Do You Know?

Puzzles are instrumental for the origin of Algebra. Leelavathi is the first puzzle book in India written by Baskaracharya (Baskara II) who lived in Maharashtra during 12th century. This book contains more interesting puzzles. The modified version of an interesting puzzle is for you.

“Two sisters were playing in a room. A pearl necklace of one of them got cut off. Out of the total pearls in the necklace, one- third fell on the floor and one-fifth fell on bed. Of the pearls falling down one sister collects one-sixth and another collects one-tenth. If 6 pearls remain in the necklace, find the total number of pearls in the necklace?”

Let the total number of pearls be \(x\).

Using the given details, we can create an equation.

\(x - \left(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{x}{5} + \frac{x}{6} + \frac{x}{10}\right) = 6\)

\(x - x\left(\frac{10}{30} + \frac{6}{30} + \frac{5}{30} + \frac{3}{30}\right) = 6\)

LCM taken for 5,6,3,10 is 30.

So, \(x\left(1 - \frac{24}{30}\right) = 6\)

\(x\left(\frac{30 - 24}{30}\right) = 6\)

\(x\left(\frac{6}{30}\right) = 6\)

\(x\left(\frac{1}{5}\right) = 6\)

\(x = 6 \times 5 = 30\) pearls
