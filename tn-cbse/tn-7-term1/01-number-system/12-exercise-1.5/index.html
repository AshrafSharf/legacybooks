Example 1.30

How many years are between 323 BC(BCE) and 1687 AD(CE)?

Solution

Years in AD(CE) are taken as positive integers and BC(BCE) as negative integers. Therefore, the difference is

\(= 1687 - (- 323) = 1687 + 323 = 2010\) years
