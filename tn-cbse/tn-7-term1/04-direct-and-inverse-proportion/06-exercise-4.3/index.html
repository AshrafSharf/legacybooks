Exercise 4.3; Miscellaneous Practice problems

Textbook pages 10 · 2 figures

  1. If the cost of 7kg of onions is ₹84 find the following
    1. Weight of the onions bought for ₹180
    2. The cost of 3 kg of onions
  2. If \(C = kd\),
    1. what is the relation between \(C\) and \(d\)?
    2. find \(k\) when \(C = 30\) and \(d = 6\)
    3. find \(C\), when \(d = 10\)
  1. Every 3 months Tamilselvan deposits ₹5000 as savings in his bank account. In how many years he can save ₹1,50,000.
  2. A printer, prints a book of 300 pages at the rate of 30 pages per minute. Then, how long will it take to print the same book if the speed of the printer is 25 pages per minute?
  3. If the cost of 6 cans of juice is ₹210, then what will be the cost of 4 cans of juice?
  4. \(x\) varies inversly as twice of \(y\). Given that when \(y = 6\), the value of \(x\) is 4. Find the value of \(x\) when \(y = 8\).
  5. A truck requires 108 liters of diesel for covering a distance of 594km. How much diesel will be required to cover a distance of 1650km?
  6. If 30 men can reap a field in 15 days, then in how many days can 20 men reap the same field?

Challenge Problems

  1. If the cost of a dozen soaps is ₹396, what will be the cost of 35 such soaps?
  2. In a school, there is 7 period a day each of 45 minutes duration. How long each period is, if the school has 9 periods a day assuming the number of hours to be the same?
  3. Cost of 105 notebooks is ₹2415. How many notebooks can be bought for ₹1863?
  4. 10 farmers can plough a field in 21 days. Find the number of days reduced if 14 farmers ploughed the same field?
  5. A flood relief camp has food stock by which 80 people can be benefited for 60 days. After 10days 20 more people have joined the camp. Calculate the number of days of food shortage due to the addition of 20 more people?
  6. Six men can complete a work in 12 days. Two days later, 6 more men joined them. How many days will they take to complete the remaining work?
