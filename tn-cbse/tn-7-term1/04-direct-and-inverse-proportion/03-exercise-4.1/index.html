Exercise 4.1

  1. Fill in the blanks
    1. If the cost of 8 apples is ₹56 then the cost of 12 apples is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    2. If the weight of one fruit box is \(3 \frac{1}{2}\) kg, then the weight of 6 such boxes is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    3. A car travels 60 km with 3 liters of petrol. If the car has to cover the distance of 200 km, it requires \(\underline{\qquad}\) liters of petrol.
    4. If the cost of 7 m cloth is ₹294, then the cost of 5 m of cloth is \(\underline{\qquad}\).
    5. If a machine in a cool drinks factory fills 600 bottles in 5 hrs, then it will fill \(\underline{\qquad}\) bottles in 3 hours.
  2. Say True or False
    1. Distance travelled by a bus and time taken are in direct proportion.
    2. Expenditure of a family to number of members of the family are in direct proportion.
    3. Number of students in a hostel and consumption of food are not in direct proportion.
    4. If Mallika walks 1 km in 20 minutes, then she can cover 3 km in 1 hour.
    5. If 12 men can dig a pond in 8 days, then 18 men can dig it in 6 days.
  1. A dozen bananas costs ₹20. What is the price of 48 bananas?
  2. A group of 21 students paid ₹840 as the entry fee for a magic show. How many students entered the magic show if the total amount paid was ₹1,680?
  3. A birthday party is arranged in third floor of a hotel. 120 people take 8 trips in a lift to go to the party hall. If 12 trips were made how many people would have attended the party?
  4. The shadow of a pole with the height of 8 m is 6m. If the shadow of another pole measured at the same time is 30m, find the height of the pole?
  5. A postman can sort out 738 letters in 6 hours. How many letters can be sorted in 9 hours?
  6. If half a meter of cloth costs ₹15. Find the cost of \(8 \frac{1}{3}\) meters of the same cloth.
  7. The weight of 72 books is 9kg. what is the weight of 40 such books? (using unitary method)
  8. Thamarai pays ₹7500 as rent for 3 months. With the same rate how much does she have to pay for 1 year? (using unitary method).
  9. Valli buys 10 pens for ₹180 and Kamala buys 8 pens for ₹96. Can you say who bought the pen cheaper? (using unitary method)
  10. A motorbike requires 2 litres of petrol to cover 100 kilometers. How many litres of petrol will be required to cover 250 kilometers? (using unitary method)

Objective type questions

  1. If the cost of 3 books is ₹90, then find the cost of 12 books.

    (i) ₹300   (ii) ₹320   (iii) ₹360   (iv) ₹400

  2. If Mani buys 5kg of potatoes for ₹75 then he can buy \(\underline{\qquad}\)kg of potatoes for ₹105.

    (i) 6   (ii) 7   (iii) 8   (iv) 5

  3. 35 cycles were produced in 5 days by a company then \(\underline{\qquad}\) cycles will be produced in 21 days.

    (i) 150   (ii) 70   (iii) 100   (iv) 147

  4. An aircraft can accommodate 280 people in 2 trips. It can take \(\underline{\qquad}\) trips to take 1400 people.

    (i) 8   (ii) 10   (iii) 9   (iv) 12

  5. Suppose 3 kg. of sugar is used to prepare sweets for 50 members, then \(\underline{\qquad}\) kg. of sugar is required for 150 members.

    (i) 9   (ii) 10   (iii) 15   (iv) 6
