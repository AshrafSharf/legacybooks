  • Parallelogram is a four sided closed shape in which opposite sides are both parallel and equal.
  • Area of the Parallelogram \(= b x h\) sq. units, where \(b =\) base ; \(h =\) height.
  • In a parallelogram if all the sides are equal then it is called Rhombus.

\(\frac{1}{2}\)

  • Area of rhombus \(= x d\times d\) sq. units, where d , d are the diagonals.
  • A parallelogram with one pair of non-parallel sides is known as a Trapezium.
  • Area of the Trapezium \(= \frac{1}{2} x h (a+b)\) sq. units. Let the distance between the two parallel sides be 'h' units.
  • If the non - parallel sides of a Trapezium are equal then it is known as an isosceles

Trapezium.
