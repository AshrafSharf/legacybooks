  1. For each statement identify the appropriate algebraic expression(s).
    1. Two more than a square number.

      \(2 + s\)   \((s + 2)^2\)   \(s^2 + 2\)   \(s^2 + 4\)   \(2s^2\)   \(2^2 s\)

    2. The sum of the squares of two consecutive numbers

      \(m^2 + n^2\)   \((m + n)^2\)   \(m^2 + 1\)   \(m^2 + (m + 1)^2\)

      \(m^2 + (m - 1)^2\)   \((m + (m + 1))^2\)   \((2m)^2 + (2m + 1)^2\)
