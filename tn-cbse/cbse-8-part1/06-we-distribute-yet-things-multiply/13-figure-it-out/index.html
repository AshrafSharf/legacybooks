  • 9.Which is larger? Find out without fully computing the product.
  • (i)\(14 \times 26\) or \(16 \times 24\)
  • (ii)\(25 \times 75\) or \(26 \times 74\)

Ans.

  • (i)Let \(a = 14 \times 26\) and \(b = 16 \times 24\)

Now \(a = (16 - 2) (24 + 2) = 16 \times 24 + 16 \times 2 - 2 \times 24 - 2 \times 2 = b + (32 - 48 - 4) = b - 20\) Hence, \(b > a\) or \(16 \times 24 > 14 \times 26\)

  • (ii)Let \(a = 25 \times 75\) and \(b = 26 \times 74\)

Now \(a = (26 - 1) (74 + 1) = 26 \times 74 + 26 - 74 - 1 = b - 49\) Hence \(b > a\) or \(26 \times 74 > 25 \times 75\)
