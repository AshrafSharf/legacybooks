Textbook pages 27–30

  • 1.Anagh mixes 600 mL of orange juice with 900 mL of apple juice to

make a fruit drink. Write the ratio of orange juice to apple juice in its simplest form.

Ans. Ratio of orange juice to apple juice is 600 : 900 Simplest form is 2 : 3

  • 2.Last year, we hired 3 buses for the school trip. We had a total of 162

students and teachers who went on that trip and all the buses were full. This year we have 204 students and teachers. How many buses will we need? Will all the buses be full?

Ans. 3 buses carried 162 students and teacher. All the buses are full so capacity of one bus \(= \frac{162}{3} = 54\) Let there be x number of buses for 204 student. Now, 3 : 162 :: x : 204

So, \(x = \frac{34}{9}\)

We need 4 buses. Number of seats in 4 buses \(= 4 \times 54 = 216\) Total number of students and teachers \(= 204\) So, \(216 - 204 = 12\) seats will remain vacant.

  • 3.The area of Delhi is 1,484 sq. km and the area of Mumbai is 550 sq.

km. The population of Delhi is approximately 30 million and that of Mumbai is 20 million people. Which city is more crowded? Why do you say so?

Ans. Calculate the number of persons in 1sq. km of both Delhi and Mumbai Number of persons per sq. km in Delhi \(= \frac{30000000}{1484} \approx 20216\) Number of persons per sq. km in Mumbai \(= \frac{20000000}{550} \approx 36,364\) Since, Number of persons per sq. km in Mumbai is more than in Delhi so, Mumbai is more crowded.

  • 4.A crane of height 155 cm has its neck and the rest of its body in the

ratio 4 : 6. For your height, if your neck and the rest of the body also had this ratio, how tall would your neck be?

Ans. The ratio of the neck and rest body of crane is 4:6.

Measure your height. Neck height \(= \frac{4}{10} \times\) your height

  • 5.Let us try an ancient problem from Līlāvatī. At that time weights

were measured in a unit named palas and niskas was a unit of money. “If \(2 \frac{1}{2}\) palas of saffron costs \(\frac{3}{7}\) niskas, O expert businessman! tell me quickly what quantity of saffron can be bought for 9 niskas?”

Ans. 52.5 palas

Hence 52.5 palas of saffron can be bought for 9 niskas

  • 6.Harmain is a 1-year-old girl. Her elder brother is 5 years old. What

will be Harmain’s age when the ratio of her age to her brother’s age is 1 : 2?

Ans. Given the age of Harmain is 1 and her brother is 5.

Let after x years, ratio of Harmain’s age to the brother be 1 : 2

\(x + 1\) : \(+ 5 = 1\) : \(2 \frac{x + 1}{x + 5} = 1 \frac{x}{2} 2x + 2 = x + 5 x = 3\)

Hence after 3 years Harmain’s age will be 4 years.

  • 7.The mass of equal volumes of gold and water are in the ratio 37 : 2.

If 1 litre of water is 1 kg in mass, what is the mass of 1 litre of gold?

Ans. Given 1lt of water \(= 1\) kg of its mass

Let 1 lt of gold \(= x\) kg of its mass Given the ratio of mass of equal volume of gold to water is 37 : 2 So 37 : 2 :: x : 1

Or \(x = \frac{37 \times 1}{2} = 18.5\) kg

Hence mass of 1 litre of gold is 18.5 kg.

  • 8.It is good farming practice to apply 10 tonnes of cow manure for 1

acre of land. A farmer is planning to grow tomatoes in a plot of size 200 ft by 500 ft. How much manure should he buy? (Please refer to the section on Unit Conversions earlier in this chapter).

Ans. We know that 1 ton \(= 1000\) kg, then 10 tonnes \(= 10,000\) kg And 1 acre \(= 43560\) sq.ft Ratio of cow manure to area of land is 10,000 : 43560 Size of plot \(= 200 \times 500 = 100,000\) sq ft. Let cow manure required be x kg Then x : 100,000 :: 10,000 : 43560

So \(x = 100,000 \times 10,000 \approx 22956.8\) kg

43560

Hence the farmer should buy 22956.8 kg or 22.9568 tonnes of cow manure.

  • 9.A tap takes 15 seconds to fill a mug of water. The volume of the mug

is 500 mL. How much time does the same tap take to fill a bucket of water if the bucket has a 10-litre capacity?

Ans. \(1 L = 1,000\) mL.

Hence time taken to fill a 10 L bucket is 300 seconds or 5 minutes.

  • 10.One acre of land costs ₹15,00,000. What is the cost of 2,400 square

feet of the same land?

Ans. 1 acre \(= 43560\) sq. ft.

Cost is ₹82645 approx.

  • 11.A tractor can plough the same area of a field 4 times faster than a

pair of oxen. A farmer wants to plough his 20-acre field. A pair of oxen takes 6 hours to plough an acre of land. How much time would it take if the farmer used a pair of oxen to plough the field? How much time would it take him if he decides to use a tractor instead?

Ans. Ratio of the time taken to plough by tractor and oxen \(= 1\) : 4 Time taken by a pair of oxen to plough 1 acre \(= 6\) hours Time taken by a tractor to plough 1 acre \(= \frac{6}{4} = 1.5\) hours Time taken by a pair of oxen to plough 20-acre of land \(= 20 \times 6\)

\(= 120\) hours

Time taken by tractor to plough 20-acre of land \(= 20 \times 1.5 = 30\) hours

  • 12.The ₹10 coin is an alloy of copper and nickel called ‘cupro-nickel’.

Copper and nickel are mixed in a 3 : 1 ratio to get this alloy. The mass of the coin is 7.74 grams. If the cost of copper is ₹906 per kg and the cost of nickel is ₹1,341 per kg, what is the cost of these metals in a ₹10 coin?

Ans. Cost of 5.805 gm copper is ₹5.26 Cost of 1000 gm nickel = ₹1341 Cost of 1.935 gm nickel = ₹2.59
