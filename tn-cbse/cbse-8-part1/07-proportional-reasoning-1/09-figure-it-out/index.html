7.6 Unit Conversions

We have noticed earlier that solving problems with proportionality often requires us to convert units from one system to another. Here are

a few important unit conversions for your reference.

Length

1 metre \(= 3.281\) feet

Area

1 square metre \(= 10.764\) square feet 1 acre \(= 43,560\) square feet 1 hectare \(= 10,000\) square metres

1 hectare \(= 2.471\) acres

Volume

1 millilitre (mL) \(= 1\) cubic centimetre (cc)

1 litre \(= 1,000\) mL or 1,000 cc

Temperature

Temperature conversion between Fahrenheit and Celsius is a bit more

complicated. \(0 C = 32 F\), and

Fahrenheit \(= \frac{9}{5} \times\) Celsius \(+ 32\)

and

Celsius \(= \frac{5}{9} \times\) (Fahrenheit \(- 32)\)

For example, 25 C is 77 F.
