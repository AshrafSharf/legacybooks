Let us arrange the powers of 4 along a line.

Can we say that 16384 (\(4^7\)) is 16 (\(4^2\)) times larger than 1,024 (\(4^5\))? Yes, since \(4^7 \div 4^5 = 4^2\).

How many times larger than \(4^{-2}\) is \(4^2\)?
