Figure it Out

  1. Represent the following numbers in the Roman system.

    1. 1222
    2. 2999
    3. 302
    4. 715

    Ans.

    1. 1222 ─ MCCXXII
    2. 2999 ─ MMCMXCIX
    3. 302 ─ CCCII
    4. 715 ─ DCCXV

Page No. 60

How will you multiply two numbers given in Roman numerals, without converting them to Hindu numerals? Try to find the product of the following pairs of landmark numbers: \(\text{V} \times \text{L}\), \(\text{L} \times \text{D}\), \(\text{V} \times \text{D}\), \(\text{VII} \times \text{IX}\).

Ans.

\(\text{V} \times \text{L} = \text{CCL}\)

\(\text{L} \times \text{D} = \text{MMM…M (25 times)}\)

\(\text{V} \times \text{D} = \text{MMD}\)

\(\text{VII} \times \text{IX} = \text{LXIII}\)
