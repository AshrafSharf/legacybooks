Figure it Out

  1. Represent the following numbers in the Mesopotamian system —

    (i) 63   (ii) 132   (iii) 200   (iv) 60   (v) 3605

Ans.

  1. \(63 = 1 \times 60 + 3\)
  2. \(132 = 2 \times 60 + 10 + 2\)
  3. \(200 = 3 \times 60 + 20\)
  4. \(60 = 1 \times 60\)
  5. \(3605 = 1 \times 3600 + 5\)

Page No. 76

Represent the following numbers using the Mayan system:

(i) 77   (ii) 100   (iii) 361   (iv) 721

Ans.

\(77 = (3) \times 20 + (17) \times 1\)

\(100 = (5) \times 20 + (0) \times 1\)

\(361 = (1) \times 360 + (0) \times 20 + (1) \times 1\)

\(721 = (2) \times 360 + (0) \times 20 + (1) \times 1\)
