Figure it Out

Textbook pages 21–22

  1. Find the cube roots of 27000 and 10648.

    Ans. (i) Cube root of \(27000 = 30\)   (ii) Cube root of \(10648 = 22\)

  2. What number will you multiply by 1323 to make it a cube number?

    Ans. 7

  3. State true or false. Explain your reasoning.
    1. The cube of any odd number is even.
    2. There is no perfect cube that ends with 8.
    3. The cube of a 2-digit number may be a 3-digit number.
    4. The cube of a 2-digit number may have seven or more digits.
    5. Cube numbers have an odd number of factors.

    Ans.

    (i) False   (ii) False   (iii) False   (iv) False   (v) False

  4. You are told that 1331 is a perfect cube. Can you guess without factorisation what its cube root is? Similarly, guess the cube roots of 4913, 12167, and 32768.

    Ans. Yes it is 11.

    \(\sqrt[3]{1331} = 11\), \(\sqrt[3]{4913} = 17\), \(\sqrt[3]{12167} = 23\), \(\sqrt[3]{32768} = 32\)

    Use your own strategies to guess.

  1. Which of the following is the greatest? Explain your reasoning.

    (i) \(67^3 - 66^3\)   (ii) \(43^3 - 42^3\)   (iii) \(67^2 - 66^2\)   (iv) \(43^2 - 42^2\)

    Ans. (i) \(67^3 - 66^3\) is the greatest.
