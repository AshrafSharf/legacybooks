Chapter 13 · Probability

Summary

Every key formula of the chapter on one page: conditional probability, independence, total probability and Bayes

Summary

The salient features of the chapter are –

Conditional Probability

The conditional probability of an event E, given the occurrence of the event F is given by

\[ P(E|F) = \frac{P(E \cap F)}{P(F)}, \quad P(F) \neq 0 \]
Properties of Conditional Probability
\[ 0 \leq P(E|F) \leq 1, \qquad P(E'|F) = 1 - P(E|F) \] \[ P((E \cup F)|G) = P(E|G) + P(F|G) - P((E \cap F)|G) \]
Multiplication Rule
\[ \begin{aligned} P(E \cap F) &= P(E)\,P(F|E), \quad P(E) \neq 0 \\ P(E \cap F) &= P(F)\,P(E|F), \quad P(F) \neq 0 \end{aligned} \]
Independent Events

If E and F are independent, then

\[ \begin{aligned} P(E \cap F) &= P(E)\,P(F) \\ P(E|F) &= P(E), \quad P(F) \neq 0 \\ P(F|E) &= P(F), \quad P(E) \neq 0 \end{aligned} \]
Theorem of Total Probability

Let \(\{E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_n\}\) be a partition of a sample space and suppose that each of \(E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_n\) has nonzero probability. Let A be any event associated with S, then

\[ P(A) = P(E_1)\,P(A|E_1) + P(E_2)\,P(A|E_2) + \ldots + P(E_n)\,P(A|E_n) \]
Bayes’ Theorem

If \(E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_n\) are events which constitute a partition of sample space S, i.e. \(E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_n\) are pairwise disjoint and \(E_1 \cup E_2 \cup \ldots \cup E_n = S\) and A be any event with nonzero probability, then

\[ P(E_i|A) = \frac{P(E_i)\,P(A|E_i)}{\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{n} P(E_j)\,P(A|E_j)} \]
