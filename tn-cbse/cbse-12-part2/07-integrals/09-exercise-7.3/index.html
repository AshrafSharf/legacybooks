Multiple Choice

Choose the correct answer in Exercises 23 and 24.

23

\( \displaystyle\int \frac{\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x}{\sin^2 x\cos^2 x}\,dx \) is equal to

  • (A) \( \tan x + \cot x + \text{C} \)
  • (B) \( \tan x + \operatorname{cosec} x + \text{C} \)
  • (C) \( -\tan x + \cot x + \text{C} \)
  • (D) \( \tan x + \sec x + \text{C} \)

(A) \( \tan x + \cot x + \text{C} \)

24

\( \displaystyle\int \frac{e^x(1 + x)}{\cos^2(e^x x)}\,dx \) equals

  • (A) \( -\cot(ex^x) + \text{C} \)
  • (B) \( \tan(xe^x) + \text{C} \)
  • (C) \( \tan(e^x) + \text{C} \)
  • (D) \( \cot(e^x) + \text{C} \)

(B) \( \tan(xe^x) + \text{C} \)
