38

\( \displaystyle\int \frac{10x^9 + 10^x\log_e 10\,dx}{x^{10} + 10^x} \) equals

  • (A) \( 10^x - x^{10} + \text{C} \)
  • (B) \( 10^x + x^{10} + \text{C} \)
  • (C) \( (10^x - x^{10})^{-1} + \text{C} \)

39

\( \displaystyle\int \frac{dx}{\sin^2 x\cos^2 x} \) equals

  • (A) \( \tan x + \cot x + \text{C} \)
  • (B) \( \tan x - \cot x + \text{C} \)
  • (C) \( \tan x\cot x + \text{C} \)
