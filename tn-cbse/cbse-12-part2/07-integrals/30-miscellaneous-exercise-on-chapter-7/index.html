Multiple Choice

Choose the correct answers in Exercises 38 to 40

38

\( \displaystyle \int \frac{dx}{e^x + e^{-x}} \) is equal to

  • (A) \( \tan^{-1}(e^x) + C \)
  • (B) \( \tan^{-1}(e^{-x}) + C \)
  • (C) \( \log(e^x - e^{-x}) + C \)
  • (D) \( \log(e^x + e^{-x}) + C \)

(A) \( \tan^{-1}(e^x) + C \)

39

\( \displaystyle \int \frac{\cos 2x}{(\sin x + \cos x)^2}\,dx \) is equal to

  • (A) \( \dfrac{-1}{\sin x + \cos x} + C \)
  • (B) \( \log|\sin x + \cos x| + C \)
  • (C) \( \log|\sin x - \cos x| + C \)
  • (D) \( \dfrac{1}{(\sin x + \cos x)^2} \)

(B) \( \log|\sin x + \cos x| + C \)

40

  • (B) \( \dfrac{a+b}{2}\displaystyle\int_a^b f(b+x)\,dx \)
  • (C) \( \dfrac{b-a}{2}\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx \)
  • (D) \( \dfrac{a+b}{2}\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx \)
