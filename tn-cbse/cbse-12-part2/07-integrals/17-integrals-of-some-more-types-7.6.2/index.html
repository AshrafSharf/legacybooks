Chapter 7 · Integrals

7.6.2 Integrals of some more types

Integration by parts unlocks three square-root integrals — √(x² − a²), √(x² + a²) and √(a² − x²)

In This Section

  • Three special standard integrals involving square roots of quadratics
  • Deriving \( \int \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\,dx \) by integration by parts, with the constant function 1 as the second function
  • The companion results for \( \sqrt{x^2 + a^2} \) and \( \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} \)
  • Completing the square to bring a general \( \sqrt{ax^2 + bx + c} \) into one of these forms

Three Special Integrals

Here, we discuss some special types of standard integrals based on the technique of integration by parts:

(i) \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\,dx \) (ii) \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{x^2 + a^2}\,dx \) (iii) \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\,dx \)

Derivation of (i)

Let \( \mathrm{I} = \displaystyle\int \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\,dx \).

Taking constant function 1 as the second function and integrating by parts, we have

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{I} &= x\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - \int \frac{1}{2}\,\frac{2x}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}}\,x\,dx \\ &= x\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - \int \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}}\,dx = x\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - \int \frac{x^2 - a^2 + a^2}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}}\,dx \\ &= x\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - \int \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\,dx - a^2 \int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}} \\ &= x\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - \mathrm{I} - a^2 \int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}} \end{aligned} \]

or

\[ 2\mathrm{I} = x\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - a^2 \int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}} \]

or

\[ \mathrm{I} = \int \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\,dx = \frac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - \frac{a^2}{2}\log\left| x + \sqrt{x^2 - a^2} \right| + \mathrm{C} \]

Similarly, integrating the other two integrals by parts, taking constant function 1 as the second function, we get the results (ii) and (iii) below.

Standard Integrals

(i) \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\,dx = \frac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - \frac{a^2}{2}\log\left| x + \sqrt{x^2 - a^2} \right| + \mathrm{C} \)

(ii) \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{x^2 + a^2}\,dx = \frac{1}{2}x\sqrt{x^2 + a^2} + \frac{a^2}{2}\log\left| x + \sqrt{x^2 + a^2} \right| + \mathrm{C} \)

(iii) \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\,dx = \frac{1}{2}x\sqrt{a^2 - x^2} + \frac{a^2}{2}\sin^{-1}\frac{x}{a} + \mathrm{C} \)

Alternatively

Integrals (i), (ii) and (iii) can also be found by making the trigonometric substitution \( x = a\sec\theta \) in (i), \( x = a\tan\theta \) in (ii) and \( x = a\sin\theta \) in (iii) respectively.

Worked Examples

Example 23

Find \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{x^2 + 2x + 5}\,dx \).

Solution Note that

\[ \int \sqrt{x^2 + 2x + 5}\,dx = \int \sqrt{(x+1)^2 + 4}\,dx \]

Put \( x + 1 = y \), so that \( dx = dy \). Then

\[ \begin{aligned} \int \sqrt{x^2 + 2x + 5}\,dx &= \int \sqrt{y^2 + 2^2}\,dy \\ &= \frac{1}{2}y\sqrt{y^2 + 4} + \frac{4}{2}\log\left| y + \sqrt{y^2 + 4} \right| + \mathrm{C} && \text{[using 7.6.2 (ii)]} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}(x+1)\sqrt{x^2 + 2x + 5} + 2\log\left| x + 1 + \sqrt{x^2 + 2x + 5} \right| + \mathrm{C} \end{aligned} \]
Example 24

Find \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{3 - 2x - x^2}\,dx \).

Solution Note that \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{3 - 2x - x^2}\,dx = \int \sqrt{4 - (x+1)^2}\,dx \)

Put \( x + 1 = y \) so that \( dx = dy \).

Thus

\[ \begin{aligned} \int \sqrt{3 - 2x - x^2}\,dx &= \int \sqrt{4 - y^2}\,dy \\ &= \frac{1}{2}y\sqrt{4 - y^2} + \frac{4}{2}\sin^{-1}\frac{y}{2} + \mathrm{C} && \text{[using 7.6.2 (iii)]} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}(x+1)\sqrt{3 - 2x - x^2} + 2\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{x+1}{2}\right) + \mathrm{C} \end{aligned} \]
Key Insight

For \( \int \sqrt{ax^2 + bx + c}\,dx \), complete the square first. The sign pattern of the result — square plus constant, square minus constant, or constant minus square — tells you which of (i), (ii), (iii) to use.
