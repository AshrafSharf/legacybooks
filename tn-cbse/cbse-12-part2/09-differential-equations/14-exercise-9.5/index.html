Chapter 9 · Differential Equations

Exercise 9.5

Find the integrating factor, multiply through, and integrate: linear equations in practice

General Solutions

For each of the differential equations given in Exercises 1 to 12, find the general solution:

1

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + 2y = \sin x \)

\( y = \dfrac{1}{5}(2\sin x - \cos x) + C\,e^{-2x} \)

2

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + 3y = e^{-2x} \)

\( y = e^{-2x} + C\,e^{-3x} \)

3

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + \dfrac{y}{x} = x^2 \)

\( xy = \dfrac{x^4}{4} + C \)

4

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + (\sec x)\,y = \tan x \left(0 \le x \lt \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) \)

\( y(\sec x + \tan x) = \sec x + \tan x - x + C \)

5

\( \cos^2 x\dfrac{dy}{dx} + y = \tan x \left(0 \le x \lt \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) \)

\( y = (\tan x - 1) + C\,e^{-\tan x} \)

6

\( x\dfrac{dy}{dx} + 2y = x^2\log x \)

\( y = \dfrac{x^2}{16}(4\log|x| - 1) + C\,x^{-2} \)

7

\( x\log x\dfrac{dy}{dx} + y = \dfrac{2}{x}\log x \)

\( y\log x = \dfrac{-2}{x}(1 + \log|x|) + C \)

8

\( (1 + x^2)\,dy + 2xy\,dx = \cot x\,dx\ (x \neq 0) \)

\( y = (1 + x^2)^{-1}\log|\sin x| + C(1 + x^2)^{-1} \)

9

\( x\dfrac{dy}{dx} + y - x + xy\cot x = 0\ (x \neq 0) \)

\( y = \dfrac{1}{x} - \cot x + \dfrac{C}{x\sin x} \)

10

\( (x + y)\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 1 \)

\( (x + y + 1) = C\,e^{y} \)

11

\( y\,dx + (x - y^2)\,dy = 0 \)

\( x = \dfrac{y^2}{3} + \dfrac{C}{y} \)

12

\( (x + 3y^2)\dfrac{dy}{dx} = y\ (y \gt 0) \)

\( x = 3y^2 + Cy \)

Particular Solutions

For each of the differential equations given in Exercises 13 to 15, find a particular solution satisfying the given condition:

13

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + 2y\tan x = \sin x;\ y = 0 \) when \( x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} \)

\( y = \cos x - 2\cos^2 x \)

14

\( (1 + x^2)\dfrac{dy}{dx} + 2xy = \dfrac{1}{1 + x^2};\ y = 0 \) when \( x = 1 \)

\( y(1 + x^2) = \tan^{-1} x - \dfrac{\pi}{4} \)

15

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} - 3y\cot x = \sin 2x;\ y = 2 \) when \( x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} \)

\( y = 4\sin^3 x - 2\sin^2 x \)

Curves from Tangent Conditions

16

Find the equation of a curve passing through the origin given that the slope of the tangent to the curve at any point \( (x, y) \) is equal to the sum of the coordinates of the point.

\( x + y + 1 = e^{x} \)

17

Find the equation of a curve passing through the point (0, 2) given that the sum of the coordinates of any point on the curve exceeds the magnitude of the slope of the tangent to the curve at that point by 5.

\( y = 4 - x - 2\,e^{x} \)

Choose the Correct Answer

18

The Integrating Factor of the differential equation \( x\dfrac{dy}{dx} - y = 2x^2 \) is

  • (A) \( e^{-x} \)
  • (B) \( e^{-y} \)
  • (C) \( \dfrac{1}{x} \)
  • (D) \( x \)

(C) \( \dfrac{1}{x} \)

19

The Integrating Factor of the differential equation \( (1 - y^2)\dfrac{dx}{dy} + yx = ay\ (-1 \lt y \lt 1) \) is

  • (A) \( \dfrac{1}{y^2 - 1} \)
  • (B) \( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{y^2 - 1}} \)
  • (C) \( \dfrac{1}{1 - y^2} \)
  • (D) \( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} \)

(D) \( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} \)
