Exercise 9.4

Practise spotting homogeneous equations and taming them with y = vx

Show Homogeneous and Solve

In each of the Exercises 1 to 10, show that the given differential equation is homogeneous and solve each of them.

1
\( (x^2 + xy)\,dy = (x^2 + y^2)\,dx \)

\( (x - y)^2 = Cx\,e^{-\frac{y}{x}} \)

2
\( y' = \dfrac{x + y}{x} \)

\( y = x\log|x| + Cx \)

3
\( (x - y)\,dy - (x + y)\,dx = 0 \)

\( \tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) = \dfrac{1}{2}\log(x^2 + y^2) + C \)

4
\( (x^2 - y^2)\,dx + 2xy\,dy = 0 \)

\( x^2 + y^2 = Cx \)

5
\( x^2\dfrac{dy}{dx} = x^2 - 2y^2 + xy \)

\( \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\log\left|\dfrac{x + \sqrt{2}y}{x - \sqrt{2}y}\right| = \log|x| + C \)

6
\( x\,dy - y\,dx = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}\,dx \)

\( y + \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = Cx^2 \)

7
\( \left\{x\cos\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) + y\sin\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)\right\}y\,dx = \left\{y\sin\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) - x\cos\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)\right\}x\,dy \)

\( xy\cos\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) = C \)

8
\( x\dfrac{dy}{dx} - y + x\sin\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) = 0 \)

\( x\left[1 - \cos\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)\right] = C\sin\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) \)

9
\( y\,dx + x\log\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)dy - 2x\,dy = 0 \)

\( cy = \log\left|\dfrac{y}{x}\right| - 1 \)

10
\( \left(1 + e^{\frac{x}{y}}\right)dx + e^{\frac{x}{y}}\left(1 - \dfrac{x}{y}\right)dy = 0 \)

\( y\,e^{\frac{x}{y}} + x = C \)

Particular Solutions

For each of the differential equations in Exercises from 11 to 15, find the particular solution satisfying the given condition:

11
\( (x + y)\,dy + (x - y)\,dx = 0;\ y = 1 \) when \( x = 1 \)

\( \log(x^2 + y^2) + 2\tan^{-1}\dfrac{y}{x} = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + \log 2 \)

12
\( x^2\,dy + (xy + y^2)\,dx = 0;\ y = 1 \) when \( x = 1 \)

\( y + 2x = 3x^2 y \)

13
\( \left[x\sin^2\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) - y\right]dx + x\,dy = 0;\ y = \dfrac{\pi}{4} \) when \( x = 1 \)

\( \cot\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) = \log|ex| \)

14
\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} - \dfrac{y}{x} + \operatorname{cosec}\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) = 0;\ y = 0 \) when \( x = 1 \)

\( \cos\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) = \log|ex| \)

15
\( 2xy + y^2 - 2x^2\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 0;\ y = 2 \) when \( x = 1 \)

\( y = \dfrac{2x}{1 - \log|x|}\ (x \neq 0,\ x \neq e) \)

Choose the Correct Answer

16
A homogeneous differential equation of the from \( \dfrac{dx}{dy} = h\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right) \) can be solved by making the substitution.
  • (A) \( y = vx \)
  • (B) \( v = yx \)
  • (C) \( x = vy \)
  • (D) \( x = v \)

(C) \( x = vy \)

17
Which of the following is a homogeneous differential equation?
  • (A) \( (4x + 6y + 5)\,dy - (3y + 2x + 4)\,dx = 0 \)
  • (B) \( (xy)\,dx - (x^3 + y^3)\,dy = 0 \)
  • (C) \( (x^3 + 2y^2)\,dx + 2xy\,dy = 0 \)
  • (D) \( y^2\,dx + (x^2 - xy - y^2)\,dy = 0 \)

(D) \( y^2\,dx + (x^2 - xy - y^2)\,dy = 0 \)
