Exercise 9.2

Verify that given functions satisfy their differential equations

Exercise 9.2

In each of the Exercises 1 to 10 verify that the given functions (explicit or implicit) is a solution of the corresponding differential equation:

1

\( y = e^x + 1 \)  :  \( y'' - y' = 0 \)

2

\( y = x^2 + 2x + \mathrm{C} \)  :  \( y' - 2x - 2 = 0 \)

3

\( y = \cos x + \mathrm{C} \)  :  \( y' + \sin x = 0 \)

4

\( y = \sqrt{1 + x^2} \)  :  \( y' = \dfrac{xy}{1 + x^2} \)

5

\( y = \mathrm{A}x \)  :  \( xy' = y \quad (x \neq 0) \)

6

\( y = x\sin x \)  :  \( xy' = y + x\sqrt{x^2 - y^2} \quad (x \neq 0 \text{ and } x \gt y \text{ or } x \lt -y) \)

7

\( xy = \log y + \mathrm{C} \)  :  \( y' = \dfrac{y^2}{1 - xy} \quad (xy \neq 1) \)

8

\( y - \cos y = x \)  :  \( (y\sin y + \cos y + x)\,y' = y \)

9

\( x + y = \tan^{-1} y \)  :  \( y^2 y' + y^2 + 1 = 0 \)

10

\( y = \sqrt{a^2 - x^2},\ x \in (-a, a) \)  :  \( x + y\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 0 \quad (y \neq 0) \)
