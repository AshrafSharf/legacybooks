Miscellaneous Exercise on Chapter 9

A mixed review: order and degree, verification, and every solution method of the chapter

Order, Degree and Verification

1

For each of the differential equations given below, indicate its order and degree (if defined).

  • (i) \( \dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} + 5x\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2 - 6y = \log x \)
  • (ii) \( \left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^3 - 4\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2 + 7y = \sin x \)
  • (iii) \( \dfrac{d^4y}{dx^4} - \sin\left(\dfrac{d^3y}{dx^3}\right) = 0 \)

(i) Order 2; Degree 1

(ii) Order 1; Degree 3

(iii) Order 4; Degree not defined

2

For each of the exercises given below, verify that the given function (implicit or explicit) is a solution of the corresponding differential equation.

  • (i) \( xy = a\,e^{x} + b\,e^{-x} + x^2 \quad : \quad x\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} + 2\dfrac{dy}{dx} - xy + x^2 - 2 = 0 \)
  • (ii) \( y = e^{x}(a\cos x + b\sin x) \quad : \quad \dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} - 2\dfrac{dy}{dx} + 2y = 0 \)
  • (iii) \( y = x\sin 3x \quad : \quad \dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} + 9y - 6\cos 3x = 0 \)
  • (iv) \( x^2 = 2y^2\log y \quad : \quad (x^2 + y^2)\dfrac{dy}{dx} - xy = 0 \)
3

Prove that \( x^2 - y^2 = c\,(x^2 + y^2)^2 \) is the general solution of differential equation \( (x^3 - 3xy^2)\,dx = (y^3 - 3x^2y)\,dy \), where \( c \) is a parameter.

General and Particular Solutions

4

Find the general solution of the differential equation \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + \sqrt{\dfrac{1 - y^2}{1 - x^2}} = 0 \).

\( \sin^{-1} y + \sin^{-1} x = C \)

5

Show that the general solution of the differential equation \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + \dfrac{y^2 + y + 1}{x^2 + x + 1} = 0 \) is given by \( (x + y + 1) = A\,(1 - x - y - 2xy) \), where A is parameter.

6

Find the equation of the curve passing through the point \( \left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) \) whose differential equation is \( \sin x\cos y\,dx + \cos x\sin y\,dy = 0 \).

\( \cos y = \dfrac{\sec x}{\sqrt{2}} \)

7

Find the particular solution of the differential equation \( (1 + e^{2x})\,dy + (1 + y^2)\,e^{x}\,dx = 0 \), given that \( y = 1 \) when \( x = 0 \).

\( \tan^{-1} y + \tan^{-1}(e^{x}) = \dfrac{\pi}{2} \)

8

Solve the differential equation \( y\,e^{\frac{x}{y}}\,dx = \left(x\,e^{\frac{x}{y}} + y^2\right)dy\ (y \neq 0) \).

\( e^{\frac{x}{y}} = y + C \)

9

Find a particular solution of the differential equation \( (x - y)(dx + dy) = dx - dy \), given that \( y = -1 \), when \( x = 0 \). (Hint: put \( x - y = t \))

\( \log|x - y| = x + y + 1 \)

10

Solve the differential equation \( \left[\dfrac{e^{-2\sqrt{x}}}{\sqrt{x}} - \dfrac{y}{\sqrt{x}}\right]\dfrac{dx}{dy} = 1\ (x \neq 0) \).

\( y\,e^{2\sqrt{x}} = (2\sqrt{x} + C) \)

11

Find a particular solution of the differential equation \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + y\cot x = 4x\operatorname{cosec} x\ (x \neq 0) \), given that \( y = 0 \) when \( x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} \).

\( y\sin x = 2x^2 - \dfrac{\pi^2}{2}\ (\sin x \neq 0) \)

12

Find a particular solution of the differential equation \( (x + 1)\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2\,e^{-y} - 1 \), given that \( y = 0 \) when \( x = 0 \).

\( y = \log\left|\dfrac{2x + 1}{x + 1}\right|,\ x \neq -1 \)

Choose the Correct Answer

13

The general solution of the differential equation \( \dfrac{y\,dx - x\,dy}{y} = 0 \) is

  • (A) \( xy = C \)
  • (B) \( x = Cy^2 \)
  • (C) \( y = Cx \)
  • (D) \( y = Cx^2 \)

(C) \( y = Cx \)

14

The general solution of a differential equation of the type \( \dfrac{dx}{dy} + P_1 x = Q_1 \) is

  • (A) \( y\,e^{\int P_1\,dy} = \int\left(Q_1 e^{\int P_1\,dy}\right)dy + C \)
  • (B) \( y\,.\,e^{\int P_1\,dx} = \int\left(Q_1 e^{\int P_1\,dx}\right)dx + C \)
  • (C) \( x\,e^{\int P_1\,dy} = \int\left(Q_1 e^{\int P_1\,dy}\right)dy + C \)
  • (D) \( x\,e^{\int P_1\,dx} = \int\left(Q_1 e^{\int P_1\,dx}\right)dx + C \)

(C) \( x\,e^{\int P_1\,dy} = \int\left(Q_1 e^{\int P_1\,dy}\right)dy + C \)

15

The general solution of the differential equation \( e^{x}\,dy + (y\,e^{x} + 2x)\,dx = 0 \) is

  • (A) \( x\,e^{y} + x^2 = C \)
  • (B) \( x\,e^{y} + y^2 = C \)
  • (C) \( y\,e^{x} + x^2 = C \)
  • (D) \( y\,e^{y} + x^2 = C \)

(C) \( y\,e^{x} + x^2 = C \)
