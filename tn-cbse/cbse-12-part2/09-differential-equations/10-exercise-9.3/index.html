Exercise 9.3 — General Solutions

For each of the differential equations in Exercises 1 to 10, find the general solution:

1

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{1 - \cos x}{1 + \cos x} \)

\( y = 2\tan\dfrac{x}{2} - x + \mathrm{C} \)

2

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = \sqrt{4 - y^2} \quad (-2 \lt y \lt 2) \)

\( y = 2\sin(x + \mathrm{C}) \)

3

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} + y = 1 \quad (y \neq 1) \)

\( y = 1 + \mathrm{A}e^{-x} \)

4

\( \sec^2 x\tan y\,dx + \sec^2 y\tan x\,dy = 0 \)

\( \tan x\tan y = \mathrm{C} \)

5

\( (e^x + e^{-x})\,dy - (e^x - e^{-x})\,dx = 0 \)

\( y = \log(e^x + e^{-x}) + \mathrm{C} \)

6

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = (1 + x^2)(1 + y^2) \)

\( \tan^{-1} y = x + \dfrac{x^3}{3} + \mathrm{C} \)

7

\( y\log y\,dx - x\,dy = 0 \)

\( y = e^{cx} \)

8

\( x^5\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -y^5 \)

\( x^{-4} + y^{-4} = \mathrm{C} \)

9

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = \sin^{-1} x \)

\( y = x\sin^{-1} x + \sqrt{1 - x^2} + \mathrm{C} \)

10

\( e^x\tan y\,dx + (1 - e^x)\sec^2 y\,dy = 0 \)

\( \tan y = \mathrm{C}(1 - e^x) \)

Particular Solutions

For each of the differential equations in Exercises 11 to 14, find a particular solution satisfying the given condition:

11

\( (x^3 + x^2 + x + 1)\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2x^2 + x \); \( y = 1 \) when \( x = 0 \)

\( y = \dfrac{1}{4}\log\left[(x + 1)^2(x^2 + 1)^3\right] - \dfrac{1}{2}\tan^{-1} x + 1 \)

12

\( x(x^2 - 1)\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 1 \); \( y = 0 \) when \( x = 2 \)

\( y = \dfrac{1}{2}\log\left(\dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2}\right) - \dfrac{1}{2}\log\dfrac{3}{4} \)

13

\( \cos\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right) = a \quad (a \in \mathbf{R}) \); \( y = 1 \) when \( x = 0 \)

\( \cos\left(\dfrac{y - 2}{x}\right) = a \)

14

\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = y\tan x \); \( y = 1 \) when \( x = 0 \)

\( y = \sec x \)

Curves and Applications

15

Find the equation of a curve passing through the point \( (0, 0) \) and whose differential equation is \( y' = e^x\sin x \).

\( 2y - 1 = e^x(\sin x - \cos x) \)

16

For the differential equation \( xy\dfrac{dy}{dx} = (x + 2)(y + 2) \), find the solution curve passing through the point \( (1, -1) \).

\( y - x + 2 = \log\left(x^2(y + 2)^2\right) \)

17

Find the equation of a curve passing through the point \( (0, -2) \) given that at any point \( (x, y) \) on the curve, the product of the slope of its tangent and \( y \) coordinate of the point is equal to the \( x \) coordinate of the point.

\( y^2 - x^2 = 4 \)

18

At any point \( (x, y) \) of a curve, the slope of the tangent is twice the slope of the line segment joining the point of contact to the point \( (-4, -3) \). Find the equation of the curve given that it passes through \( (-2, 1) \).

\( (x + 4)^2 = y + 3 \)

19

The volume of spherical balloon being inflated changes at a constant rate. If initially its radius is 3 units and after 3 seconds it is 6 units. Find the radius of balloon after \( t \) seconds.

\( (63t + 27)^{\frac{1}{3}} \)

20

In a bank, principal increases continuously at the rate of \( r\% \) per year. Find the value of \( r \) if Rs 100 double itself in 10 years \( (\log_e 2 = 0.6931) \).

\( 6.93\% \)

21

In a bank, principal increases continuously at the rate of 5% per year. An amount of Rs 1000 is deposited with this bank, how much will it worth after 10 years \( (e^{0.5} = 1.648) \).

\( \text{Rs } 1648 \)

22

In a culture, the bacteria count is 1,00,000. The number is increased by 10% in 2 hours. In how many hours will the count reach 2,00,000, if the rate of growth of bacteria is proportional to the number present?

\( \dfrac{2\log 2}{\log\left(\dfrac{11}{10}\right)} \)

Multiple Choice

23

The general solution of the differential equation \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = e^{x + y} \) is

  • (A) \( e^x + e^{-y} = \mathrm{C} \)
  • (B) \( e^x + e^y = \mathrm{C} \)
  • (C) \( e^{-x} + e^y = \mathrm{C} \)
  • (D) \( e^{-x} + e^{-y} = \mathrm{C} \)

\( \text{(A) } e^x + e^{-y} = \mathrm{C} \)
