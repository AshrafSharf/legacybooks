9.4 Methods of Solving First Order, First Degree Differential Equations

Three techniques for solving equations of the form dy/dx = F(x, y)

In This Section

  • The three solution methods discussed in this chapter

Three Methods

In this section we shall discuss three methods of solving first order first degree differential equations.

Key Insight — Roadmap

A first order, first degree differential equation can be written as \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \mathrm{F}(x, y)\). The three methods that follow each handle a special form of \(\mathrm{F}\):

  1. 9.4.1 Differential equations with variables separable
  2. 9.4.2 Homogeneous differential equations
  3. 9.4.3 Linear differential equations
