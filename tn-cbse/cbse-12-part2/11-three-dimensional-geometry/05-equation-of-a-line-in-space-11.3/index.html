11.3 Equation of a Line in Space

What it takes to pin down a line in three dimensions

In This Section

  • Moving from lines in the plane (Class XI) to lines in space
  • The two ways a line is uniquely determined
  • A roadmap for the vector and Cartesian equations that follow

When is a Line Fixed?

We have studied equation of lines in two dimensions in Class XI, we shall now study the vector and cartesian equations of a line in space.

A line is uniquely determined if
  1. it passes through a given point and has given direction, or
  2. it passes through two given points.
Key Insight

Case (ii) reduces to case (i): two points A and B give the direction \(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}\), so a point and a direction is really all we ever need.
