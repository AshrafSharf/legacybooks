Chapter 8 · Application of Integrals

Summary

Two formulas for area: vertical strips along x, horizontal strips along y

Summary

Area using vertical strips

The area of the region bounded by the curve \( y = f(x) \), \(x\)-axis and the lines \( x = a \) and \( x = b \) \( (b \gt a) \) is given by the formula:

\[ \text{Area} = \int_a^b y\,dx = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx. \]
Area using horizontal strips

The area of the region bounded by the curve \( x = \phi(y) \), \(y\)-axis and the lines \( y = c \), \( y = d \) is given by the formula:

\[ \text{Area} = \int_c^d x\,dy = \int_c^d \phi(y)\,dy. \]
