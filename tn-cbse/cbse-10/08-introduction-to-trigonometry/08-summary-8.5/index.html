Chapter 8 · Introduction to Trigonometry

8.5 Summary

In this chapter, you have studied the following points :

  • 1.In a right triangle ABC, right-angled at B,
    \(\sin \text{A} = \dfrac{\text{side opposite to angle A}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, \ \cos \text{A} = \dfrac{\text{side adjacent to angle A}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\)
    \(\tan \text{A} = \dfrac{\text{side opposite to angle A}}{\text{side adjacent to angle A}}\).
  • 2.\(\operatorname{cosec} \text{A} = \dfrac{1}{\sin \text{A}}; \ \sec \text{A} = \dfrac{1}{\cos \text{A}}; \ \tan \text{A} = \dfrac{1}{\cot \text{A}}, \ \tan \text{A} = \dfrac{\sin \text{A}}{\cos \text{A}}\).
  • 3.If one of the trigonometric ratios of an acute angle is known, the remaining trigonometric ratios of the angle can be easily determined.
  • 4.The values of trigonometric ratios for angles \(0^{\circ}, 30^{\circ}, 45^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}\) and \(90^{\circ}\).
  • 5.The value of sin A or cos A never exceeds 1, whereas the value of sec A \((0^{\circ} \le \text{A} \lt 90^{\circ})\) or cosec A \((0^{\circ} \lt \text{A} \le 90^{\circ})\) is always greater than or equal to 1.
  • 6.\(\sin^{2} \text{A} + \cos^{2} \text{A} = 1\),
    \(\sec^{2} \text{A} - \tan^{2} \text{A} = 1\) for \(0^{\circ} \le \text{A} \lt 90^{\circ}\),
    \(\operatorname{cosec}^{2} \text{A} = 1 + \cot^{2} \text{A}\) for \(0^{\circ} \lt \text{A} \le 90^{\circ}\).
