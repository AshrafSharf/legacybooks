Chapter 3 · Perimeter and Area

Summary

Textbook pages 18

  • The perimeter of any closed figure is the total length of its boundary.
  • Perimeter of the rectangle, \(P= 2 x\) (l + b) units.
  • Perimeter of the square, \(P = (4 x\) s) units.
  • Perimeter of the triangle, \(P =\) (a \(+ b +\) c) units.
  • Perimeter of the shape with equal sides = Number of sides x Length of a side.
  • The area is the measure of the region/surface occupied by a closed figure.
  • Area of a rectangle, \(A =\) length x breadth = (l x b) sq. units.
  • Area of a square, \(A =\) side x side = (s x s) sq. units.
  • Area of the right angled triangle, \(A = \frac{1}{2}\) (b x h) sq. units.
  • The perimeter of a combined shape is the sum of the length of all outer sides of the shapes.
  • The area of a combined shape is the sum of all the areas of regular/simpler shapes by which the combined shape is formed.
