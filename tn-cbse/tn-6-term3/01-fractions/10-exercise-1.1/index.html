Exercise 1.1

Textbook pages 19–20 · 2 figures

  1. Fill in the blanks
    1. \(7\frac{3}{4} + 6\frac{1}{2} = \underline{\qquad}\)
    2. The sum of a whole number and a proper fraction is called \(\underline{\qquad}\)
    3. \(5\frac{1}{2} - 3\frac{1}{3} = \underline{\qquad}\)
    4. \(8 \div \frac{1}{2} = \underline{\qquad}\)
    5. The number which has its own reciprocal is \(\underline{\qquad}\)
  2. Say True or False
    1. \(3\frac{1}{2}\) can be written as \(3 + \frac{1}{2}\).
    2. The sum of any two proper fractions is always an improper fraction.
    3. The mixed fraction of \(\frac{13}{4}\) is \(3\frac{1}{4}\).
    4. The reciprocal of an improper fraction is always a proper fraction.
    5. \(3\frac{1}{4} \times 3\frac{1}{4} = 9\frac{1}{16}\)
  3. Answer the following:
    1. Find the sum of \(\frac{1}{7}\) and \(\frac{3}{9}\).
    2. What is the total of \(3\frac{1}{3}\) and \(4\frac{1}{6}\)?
    3. Simplify : \(1\frac{3}{5} + 5\frac{4}{7}\).
    4. Find the difference between \(\frac{8}{9}\) and \(\frac{2}{7}\).
    5. Subtract \(1\frac{3}{5}\) from \(2\frac{1}{3}\).
    6. Simplify : \(7\frac{2}{7} - 3\frac{4}{21}\).
  4. Convert mixed fractions into improper fractions and vice versa:
    1. \(3\frac{7}{18}\)
    2. \(\frac{99}{7}\)
    3. \(\frac{47}{6}\)
    4. \(12\frac{1}{9}\)
  5. Multiply the following:
    1. \(\frac{2}{3} \times 6\)
    2. \(8\frac{1}{3} \times 5\)
    3. \(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{4}{5}\)
    4. \(3\frac{5}{7} \times 1\frac{1}{13}\)
  6. Divide the following:
    1. \(\frac{3}{7} \div 4\)
    2. \(\frac{4}{3} \div \frac{5}{9}\)
    3. \(4\frac{1}{5} \div 3\frac{3}{4}\)
    4. \(9\frac{2}{3} \div 1\frac{2}{3}\)
  7. Gowri purchased \(3\frac{1}{2}\,kg\) of tomatoes, \(\frac{3}{4}\,kg\) of brinjal and \(1\frac{1}{4}\,kg\) of onion. What is the total weight of the vegetables she bought?
  8. An oil tin contains \(3\frac{3}{4}\) litres of oil of which \(2\frac{1}{2}\) litres of oil is used. How much oil is left over?
  9. Nilavan can walk \(4\frac{1}{2}\,km\) in an hour. How much distance will he cover in \(3\frac{1}{2}\) hours?
  10. Ravi bought a curtain of length \(15\frac{3}{4}\,m\). If he cut the curtain into small pieces each of length \(2\frac{1}{4}\,m\), then how many small curtains will he get?

Objective Type Questions

  1. Which of the following statement is incorrect?
    1. \(\frac{1}{2} > \frac{1}{3}\)
    2. \(\frac{7}{8} > \frac{6}{7}\)
    3. \(\frac{8}{9} < \frac{9}{10}\)
    4. \(\frac{10}{11} < \frac{9}{10}\)
  2. The difference between \(\frac{3}{7}\) and \(\frac{2}{9}\) is
    1. \(\frac{13}{63}\)
    2. \(\frac{1}{9}\)
    3. \(\frac{1}{7}\)
    4. \(\frac{9}{16}\)
  3. The reciprocal of \(\frac{53}{17}\) is
    1. \(\frac{53}{17}\)
    2. \(5\frac{3}{17}\)
    3. \(\frac{17}{53}\)
    4. \(3\frac{5}{17}\)
  4. If \(\frac{6}{7} = \frac{A}{49}\), then the value of A is
    1. 42
    2. 36
    3. 25
    4. 48
  5. Pugazh has been given four choices for his pocket money by his father. Which of the choices should he take in order to get the maximum money?
    1. \(\frac{2}{3}\) of ₹150
    2. \(\frac{3}{5}\) of ₹150
    3. \(\frac{4}{5}\) of ₹150
    4. \(\frac{1}{5}\) of ₹150
