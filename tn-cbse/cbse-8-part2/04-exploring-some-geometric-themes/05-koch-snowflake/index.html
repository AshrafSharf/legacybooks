Koch Snowflake

Textbook pages 4 · 4 figures

The Koch Snowflake is another fractal, named after the Swedish mathematician Von Koch, who first described it in 1904. We have already encountered this fractal in Grade 6, Ganita Prakash.

Step 0 Step 1 Step 2

Koch Snowflake

To generate it, we start with an equilateral triangle. Each side is

  1. divided into 3 equal parts, and
  2. an equilateral triangle is raised over the middle part, and then the middle part is removed. Effectively, each side gets replaced by a ‘bump’ - shaped structure . This procedure is repeated on the sides of the new resulting shape, and so on.
